🗺️ Our island adventure

A full day exploring Zanzibar beyond Stone Town

"Swimming in that cave — the light coming through the opening, the water so clear and calm, the sense of discovery — that's the moment that sums up what we love about travel. It was pure magic."

A full-day island tour takes you beyond Stone Town and into the heart of Zanzibar's diverse landscapes and communities. The journey itself is part of the adventure — the roads are famously potholed, but that's all part of the authentic island experience. You bounce along in a local minibus or jeep, watching the scenery change and getting a sense of everyday Zanzibar beyond the tourist areas.

The tour typically takes you through different parts of the island — rural villages, fishing communities, agricultural areas, stretches of coastline. You see how people actually live on Zanzibar. You visit small communities and stop at places that aren't in the guidebooks. It's a fuller picture of the island than you get from just staying in Stone Town.

And then there's the cave swim — the crown jewel of the whole experience. The cave is a natural rock formation on the island, and the swimming area inside is absolutely stunning. The light filters through the cave opening, illuminating the water in shades of turquoise and deep blue. The water is calm and perfectly clear. You wade or swim in, surrounded by rock walls, with the sound of water and the calls of birds echoing around you. It's one of those genuinely magical travel moments — the kind that stays with you forever.

What makes it so special is the combination of natural wonder and the sense of discovery. It's not a crowded tourist spot — it feels like you're uncovering something genuine and beautiful. By the end of the day, when you return to Stone Town, you've seen a completely different side of Zanzibar and had an experience that was uniquely yours.

📋 What to bring on an island tour

Packing for a full-day island adventure

Sun protection

High SPF sunscreen is absolutely essential — especially for the cave swim. You'll be out in the sun most of the day, and there's limited shade. Bring more sunscreen than you think you need and reapply frequently.

Water and hydration

Bring plenty of water to stay hydrated throughout the day. The heat and the activity will dehydrate you quickly. A reusable water bottle that you can refill is ideal.

Swimwear and waterproof items

Obviously you'll need swimwear for the cave swim. A waterproof phone pouch or dry bag is essential if you want to keep your phone safe and capture photos. A lightweight rash guard provides extra sun protection and is more comfortable than trying to reapply sunscreen constantly.

Modest clothing

Wear loose, breathable clothing that covers your shoulders. Long trousers or lightweight leggings are more respectful when visiting communities on the island, and they also protect your legs from the sun during the journey.

Snacks

Most tours include lunch, but bringing some extra snacks is a good idea. Energy bars, fruit or nuts are helpful when you need a quick boost.

Hat and sunglasses

A wide-brimmed hat protects your face and neck from intense sun. Good sunglasses protect your eyes and make being out all day more comfortable.

Comfortable shoes or sandals

You'll be walking on the cave rocks and exploring various locations. Sandals with good grip, or lightweight hiking shoes, are ideal. Avoid flip-flops — you need secure footwear.

Towel or quick-dry fabric

A small, lightweight towel or quick-dry travel towel is useful for the cave swim. Most tours don't provide them, so bring your own.

Cash

Bring cash for tips, any meals not included in the tour, or if you want to buy souvenirs or support local communities you visit.

📋 Practical information

Planning your island tour

Duration

A full-day island tour typically runs from early morning (around 7–8 AM) until late afternoon or early evening (around 4–5 PM). That's a solid 8–9 hours of activity, so it's a full commitment but worth every minute.

Best time to visit

Island tours can be done year-round, but they're best during the dry seasons (January–February and June–October) when the roads are more navigable and weather is more predictable.

What's included

Most tours include hotel pickup and drop-off, transportation, a guide, lunch, and the cave swim. Check what's included when you book — some tours offer extras like stops at specific villages or attractions.

The roads

Yes, the roads are potholed. Yes, it gets bumpy. But that's part of the adventure and the authentic Zanzibar experience. Local drivers navigate it all with impressive skill and good humour. Go with it and enjoy the journey.

Group size

Tours vary from small groups (4–6 people) to larger groups (15–20+). Smaller groups tend to feel more personal and allow more flexibility, but larger groups can be more cost-effective. Choose based on what appeals to you.

Cost

Full-day island tours typically range from $50–$100 USD per person depending on what's included. Tours including more meals or smaller group sizes may cost more.

Is it worth it?

Absolutely. The cave swim alone is worth the entire day, and the experience of seeing a different side of Zanzibar is enriching and memorable. It's one of the experiences we recommend most highly to anyone visiting the island.
