A trip that surprised us

Turin — we went for the markets and stayed for the city

Italy's seriously underrated winter city break

“We went for the Christmas markets and stayed for the city.”

Our December 2023 weekend in Turin wasn't quite the Christmas-market trip we'd imagined — because the city itself stole the show. Piazza Castello, Palazzo Madama, miles of elegant arcades, brilliant shopping, Irish coffees and some of the best gluten-free pizza we've ever eaten.

Inside Rome — but a country of its own

Vatican City — art, history and one unforgettable Easter

Pope John Paul II's final Easter, 2005

“One of those moments you never forget.”

We've visited Vatican City more than once, but one visit stands apart. In 2005 we were in St Peter's Square for what became Pope John Paul II's final Easter. The scale of the crowd, the atmosphere and the sense of history made it a travel memory that has never left us.

Common questions

Italy guide FAQ

Which Italy guides are live?
The Italy hub currently links to Rome, Venice, Verona and Turin. Vatican City also has its own full guide because it is an independent state within Rome.
Is Turin worth visiting in winter?
Yes. We went in December 2023 expecting the Christmas markets to be the big draw, but the city itself was the highlight — grand piazzas, arcaded streets, excellent food and a brilliant winter atmosphere.
How did you find gluten-free food in Turin?
Extremely well. We found outstanding gluten-free pizza and had no difficulty finding options. For anyone with coeliac disease, we'd still recommend checking preparation and cross-contamination with each restaurant before ordering.
Is Vatican City part of a Rome trip?
Absolutely — geographically it sits inside Rome, but Vatican City is a separate independent state and warrants proper time of its own. Our dedicated Vatican guide covers the practicalities and our personal visits.
Can I explore Vatican City before I travel?
Yes. We've linked Drive From Home's Vatican City virtual experience and written guide above so you can have a look around before your real trip.

Which Italy are you in the mood for?

Ancient Rome, Venice on the water, romantic Verona or Turin with a coffee and a plate of very good food — pick a city and start planning.
