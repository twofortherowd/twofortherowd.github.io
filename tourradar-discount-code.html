The TourRadar+ savings program

How TourRadar+ works

TourRadar+ is TourRadar's free loyalty program. Every account is automatically enrolled — no forms, no codes, no minimum spend. Your saving is applied automatically at checkout when you're signed in. Which level you're on depends on how many bookings you've made:

Level 1
up to 3%
0 bookings — new members
Level 2
up to 5%
1 to 3 confirmed bookings
Level 3
up to 7%
4 or more confirmed bookings
The exact saving varies by tour and operator, which is why it's always shown as "up to". Levels never expire from inactivity — only bookings move you up, and a cancelled booking comes off your count.

What happened to the €50 first-time discount?

The old €50 first-time credit has been retired and replaced by TourRadar+. In practice, this is a better deal for most people: you get a saving on every booking, not just the first, and there's no minimum spend to unlock it. What is TourRadar+?
TourRadar+ is TourRadar's free loyalty program. Every new account is automatically enrolled — no forms, no codes, no minimum spend. Level 1 saves up to 3%, Level 2 up to 5%, and Level 3 up to 7%, applied automatically at checkout when you're signed in.
How do I level up on TourRadar+?
Levels move with confirmed bookings only. Level 1 is 0 bookings, Level 2 is 1 to 3 confirmed bookings, and Level 3 is 4 or more. Levels never expire from inactivity — but if a booking is cancelled, it comes off your count.
