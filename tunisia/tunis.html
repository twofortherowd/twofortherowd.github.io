🏖️ Our 2009 Coastal Memories

What we genuinely remember

Beach days, souks and our first taste of Tunisia

"The warmth of the Tunisian people is one of the things that stayed with us most from our 2009 trip."

We stayed somewhere on the Tunisian coast in 2009, although after all these years we haven't been able to pin down the exact resort or hotel. What we do remember clearly is the Mediterranean setting: sunshine, swimming and relaxed beach time before a very different adventure took us south.

We also remember visiting souks and soaking up the atmosphere of Tunisia beyond the beach. The colours, bustle and experience of wandering through the market streets are among the memories that have lasted — even if the exact location of those souks has disappeared from memory.

Tunisia also stood out to us as excellent value. It gave us the beach-holiday side of the trip we wanted while leaving room for the history, culture and southern desert adventure that became the most memorable part of the holiday.

What we won't do is turn a seventeen-year-old memory into a precise itinerary. We haven't confirmed that our coastal base was Tunis or that the souks we visited were the Medina of Tunis. The guide below therefore treats Tunis as a destination to explore today, rather than pretending every Tunis attraction was part of our personal 2009 itinerary.

🕌 Exploring Tunis today

Tunis, the medina and the coast

Tunis makes a useful base for exploring the capital and nearby sights. The historic Medina of Tunis is the city's great old quarter, while Carthage and the blue-and-white village of Sidi Bou Said are among the best-known places in the wider Tunis area. These are ideas for travellers planning a trip today — not a claim that they were all part of our own 2009 itinerary.

🕌
Medina of Tunis

Explore the historic old city, its lanes, souks and traditional architecture.

🏛️
Carthage

Discover the archaeological remains of one of the Mediterranean's great ancient cities.

💙
Sidi Bou Said

A distinctive blue-and-white coastal village overlooking the Mediterranean near Tunis.

Tunis & Coastal Tunisia FAQ

Did you stay in Tunis on your 2009 trip?
We stayed at a coastal resort in Tunisia, but more than fifteen years later we haven't been able to confirm the exact resort or hotel. That's why we separate our own coastal memories from the general Tunis information on this page.
Did you visit the Medina of Tunis?
We remember visiting souks during the trip, but we haven't confirmed that the souks we visited were specifically the Medina of Tunis. Rather than guess, we treat the Medina here as a place for travellers to explore today.
What can you see around Tunis today?
The historic Medina of Tunis, the archaeological site of Carthage and the coastal village of Sidi Bou Said are among the best-known places in and around Tunis. Check current opening arrangements and official travel advice before your visit.
Can you combine the coast with a trip into southern Tunisia?
That's exactly what we did in 2009. We combined our coastal holiday with an organised overnight excursion into southern Tunisia, including El Jem, traditional troglodyte dwellings and a Sahara experience. You can read that story on our Southern Tunisia & Sahara page.
Where should I check current Tunisia travel information?
Entry requirements, transport options and travel advice can change. Check current official travel advice and entry requirements for your nationality before booking or travelling.

Planning your own Tunis trip?
