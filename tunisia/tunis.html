🏖️ Our 2009 Tunis & Coastal Tunisia Story

Mediterranean days, Tunis and the souks

Our first taste of Tunisia

Tunis gave us our first real taste of Tunisia beyond the beach — colourful, lively and full of atmosphere.

Our 2009 holiday began with the relaxed side of Tunisia: sunshine, swimming and time by the Mediterranean. It was the perfect start to a trip that would eventually take us far beyond the coast and into the landscapes of Southern Tunisia.

We also spent time in Tunis, exploring the city and its souks. The bustle, colours and atmosphere were a complete contrast to the slower pace of the coast and gave us a glimpse of another side of Tunisia.

One of the things that stood out throughout the trip was the warmth of the Tunisian people, along with the excellent value that made Tunisia such an appealing Mediterranean destination.

That combination of coast and city made a brilliant introduction to the country — before our adventure changed completely as we headed south towards El Jem, Douz and the Sahara.

🕌 Exploring Tunis today

Tunis, the medina and the coast

Tunis makes a useful base for exploring the capital and nearby sights. The historic Medina of Tunis is the city's great old quarter, while Carthage and the blue-and-white village of Sidi Bou Said are among the best-known places in the wider Tunis area. Together they make Tunis a fascinating base for history, culture and Mediterranean scenery.

🕌

Explore the historic old city, its lanes, souks and traditional architecture.

A distinctive blue-and-white coastal village overlooking the Mediterranean near Tunis.

Tunis & Coastal Tunisia FAQ

Did you visit Tunis on your 2009 trip?
Yes. We spent time in Tunis during our 2009 Tunisia holiday, exploring the city and its souks alongside the coastal part of our trip.
What are some of the highlights around Tunis?
The historic Medina of Tunis, the archaeological site of Carthage and the blue-and-white coastal village of Sidi Bou Said are among the best-known places to explore in and around the capital.
Can you combine Tunis and the coast with Southern Tunisia?
Absolutely. That's how we experienced Tunisia in 2009 — combining the Mediterranean coast and Tunis with an organised overnight journey south through El Jem, Douz, traditional troglodyte dwellings and the desert.
Where should I check current Tunisia travel information?
Entry requirements, transport options and travel advice can change, so check current official travel advice and entry requirements before travelling.
