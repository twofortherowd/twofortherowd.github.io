County Monaghan —
Rolling Hills, Poetic Landscapes & Hidden Treasures

A county of beautiful drumlins, traditional villages, fascinating heritage and plenty of unexpected discoveries.

Monaghan might not have Atlantic cliffs, but it has character, charm and some wonderfully peaceful scenery.

It's famous for its rolling drumlin hills, lakes and farmland, and it's the birthplace of Patrick Kavanagh, whose poetry captured the beauty and the struggle of rural Irish life.

Add forest parks, historic estates, traditional villages and a celebrated lace-making tradition, and there's a lot more to Monaghan than meets the eye. It's ideal for a relaxed weekend away.

🧭 A quick note from Nicola and Podge: this is a researched guide rather than a diary of our own visits. When we explore and film in Monaghan, our own videos and first-hand tips will be added here, just like on our other county guides.

🛂 Near the border

Monaghan borders Armagh, Tyrone and Fermanagh. Cross into Northern Ireland and prices change to pounds sterling (£) and speed limits to miles per hour. There are no border checks.

Forests, estates & north Monaghan
Forest park · Family

🌲 Rossmore Forest Park

Woodland trails, lakes, the ruins of Rossmore Castle and plenty of space for families, just outside Monaghan town.

Why visit? Families, walking and nature.

Estate · Village

🏰 Castle Leslie & Glaslough

A grand country estate beside the pretty village of Glaslough, home to the Leslie family for centuries and famous for its luxury hospitality.

Why visit? Historic estates, gardens and countryside.

ℹ️ Some estate experiences are for staying guests or need booking.
Museum

🏛️ Monaghan County Museum

The county's award-winning museum, now in its new home at the Peace Campus in Monaghan town, covering archaeology, local history and the border.

Why visit? History and culture.

📅 Check opening times before you go.
Town · Monastic site

🗼 Clones

A historic town with a round tower, a carved high cross and early Christian roots, and its own lace tradition.

Why visit? History and traditional town atmosphere.

Upland · Bog

🥾 Sliabh Beagh

Upland bog and walking country on the border with Tyrone and Fermanagh.

Why visit? Walking and big skies.

Around the county
Kavanagh country, lace & lakes
Literature

✒️ Patrick Kavanagh Centre, Inniskeen

Discover the life and work of one of Ireland's greatest poets in the landscape that shaped him.

Why visit? Literature and cultural history.

Craft heritage

🧵 Carrickmacross Lace Gallery

See the delicate Carrickmacross lace, a tradition that has dressed royalty, and learn how it's made.

Why visit? Craft heritage and history.

Lake park

🌊 Lough Muckno & Castleblayney

A lakeside park with walks and water views beside Castleblayney, a town with a strong music heritage.

Why visit? Families, nature and local culture.

Church

⛪ St Peter's Church, Laragh

A small 19th-century church tucked into a wooded glen near Carrickmacross, with an unusual and very photogenic exterior.

Why visit? Unusual architecture.

Wetlands

🦆 Ballybay Wetlands

An important habitat for wildlife and birdwatching.

Why visit? Nature and birdwatching.

History, myth & mystery
Myths, legends & history

History and legend, clearly labelled.

Literature

✒️ Patrick Kavanagh

Kavanagh grew up on a small farm at Mucker, near Inniskeen. Poems like The Great Hunger and Shancoduff turned these small fields into some of the most famous landscapes in Irish poetry. He's buried in Inniskeen.

Craft

🧵 Carrickmacross lace

The lace tradition began in the 1820s and grew into an industry that helped families survive hard times. Its fame spread internationally, and it's still made today.

Geology

⛰️ The drumlins

Monaghan's rolling 'basket of eggs' hills were shaped by glaciers moving across the land in the last Ice Age.

History

⛪ Clones and St Tighernach

St Tighernach founded a monastery at Clones in the sixth century. Its round tower and high cross still stand in the town.

Common questions
County Monaghan FAQ
Where was Patrick Kavanagh from?
Inniskeen in south Monaghan. The Patrick Kavanagh Centre there tells his story.
Can you visit Castle Leslie if you're not staying?
Some experiences are for staying guests or need booking, so check with the estate before you go.
Is Monaghan in Northern Ireland?
No, Monaghan is in the Republic (euro), although it's one of the nine counties of Ulster and borders Northern Ireland.
