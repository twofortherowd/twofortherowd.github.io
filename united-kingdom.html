Common questions

UK travel FAQ

How do I get from Ireland to the UK?
Flights from Dublin, Cork or Shannon to Edinburgh, Manchester, London, Cardiff and Belfast are all straightforward and relatively affordable. Ferries operate from Dublin to Holyhead (Wales) and Belfast is just a short flight or bus journey from the Republic. For Glasgow and Edinburgh, direct flights from Dublin are frequent.
Which UK country should I visit first?
It depends what you're after. London is unmissable for theatre and history. The Scottish Highlands are extraordinary for scenery. Belfast is brilliant for food and culture. Cardiff for a compact city break. Start with whichever appeals most — they're all easily connected and well worth multiple visits.
Do I need a car to explore the UK?
No, but it depends where you're going. Cities like London, Edinburgh and Belfast have excellent public transport. For the Scottish Highlands, Causeway Coast and more remote areas, having your own wheels gives you freedom and flexibility. Train travel between cities is excellent and often more relaxing than driving.
How long should I spend in the UK?
Even a week gives you meaningful time in two or three countries. A fortnight lets you do a proper mix — perhaps Edinburgh and the Highlands, London, and Belfast with the Causeway Coast. We've had wonderful trips of a week, and equally brilliant extended stays exploring one region in depth.
