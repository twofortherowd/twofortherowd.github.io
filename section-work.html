Why brands work with us

We don't sound like an advert.

Our audience follows because they know we'll tell them the honest version — what worked, what didn't, what we'd pay for ourselves and what we'd skip next time. That credibility is the bit we protect, because it's the reason recommendations actually mean something when we make them.

We also speak directly to travellers who need reliable gluten-free information, a group that is still badly underserved in mainstream travel content.
