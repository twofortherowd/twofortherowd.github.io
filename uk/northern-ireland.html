Common questions

Northern Ireland FAQ

Is Belfast worth visiting from Ireland?
Absolutely — Belfast is a brilliant city and incredibly easy to get to from the Republic. The black cab political tours are one of the most fascinating and thought-provoking travel experiences you can have on the island of Ireland. The food scene has improved enormously and the Game of Thrones filming locations on the Causeway Coast are world class.
Is the Game of Thrones tour in Northern Ireland worth it?
Yes — genuinely brilliant even if you're not a massive Game of Thrones fan. The Dark Hedges, the Causeway Coast, Cushendun Caves and the other filming locations are all stunning in their own right. The landscape of the Antrim Coast is extraordinary and the GoT connection gives the whole day an extra layer of excitement.
How long should I spend in Northern Ireland?
Belfast deserves at least two days to explore the city properly. For the Causeway Coast, a full day driving and exploring is ideal. A long weekend gives you time for both Belfast and a Causeway Coast day trip.
Do I need a car in Northern Ireland?
Belfast is walkable and has good public transport. For exploring the Causeway Coast, a car gives you flexibility to stop wherever you want and explore smaller roads. Alternatively, organized tours take care of driving while you enjoy the scenery.
