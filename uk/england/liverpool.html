Liverpool in a Day our extreme day trip! 🎸✈️

One city, one day, two exhausted travellers — and absolutely no regrets! Who says you need a weekend away to explore a new city? On Saturday, 26 September 2026, we headed off on another Two for the Row'd adventure, this time taking on the legendary city of Liverpool in just one day.

With an early morning flight from Dublin and a late-night flight home, we had roughly 14 hours to soak up as much of Liverpool as possible. And, as usual, we weren't going to waste a minute!

🎶 Following in the footsteps of The Beatles

Liverpool and The Beatles go hand in hand, so naturally, we had to dive headfirst into the city's musical history.

Our adventure included the famous Magical Mystery Tour, taking us around some of the places that shaped John, Paul, George and Ringo's early lives. From the legendary Penny Lane to the iconic Strawberry Field, it was a fantastic way to experience the stories behind some of the world's most famous songs.

And of course, no Beatles-themed trip would be complete without a visit to the Cavern Club, the legendary venue where the Fab Four played so many of their early gigs. Stepping inside felt like walking straight into a piece of music history!

🥂 Afternoon tea, Beatles style!

We even managed to squeeze in a rather civilised afternoon tea at The Beatles Story, complete with a little sparkle!

It was a lovely opportunity to put our feet up, enjoy a treat and recharge before heading back out to explore. And with gluten-free options available, it was a welcome addition to our jam-packed itinerary.

🚌 Seeing Liverpool from the top deck

To make the most of our limited time, we also hopped aboard a Liverpool sightseeing bus tour.

There's something brilliant about seeing a city from the top deck of an open-top bus, especially when you're trying to pack an entire weekend's worth of sightseeing into a single day!

Liverpool is a city bursting with character, incredible architecture, music, culture and maritime history. From its famous waterfront to its bustling streets, there's plenty to see beyond The Beatles.

🏆 Would we do it again?

In a heartbeat!

Liverpool proved that you don't need loads of annual leave, expensive hotel stays or an elaborate itinerary to have a fantastic adventure abroad.

Yes, it was a long day. Yes, we covered serious ground. And yes, we were fit for the bed by the time we got home! But that's exactly what our extreme day trips are all about: making the most of every opportunity to travel, even when you've only got one day to spare.

Our verdict: Liverpool is absolutely worth the trip, whether you've got one day or an entire weekend. If you're looking for your next adventure, don't overlook this brilliant city just across the Irish Sea.
❓ Liverpool day trip FAQ
Can you do Liverpool in a day from Dublin?
Yes. We took an early morning flight from Dublin and a late-night flight home, which gave us roughly 14 hours in Liverpool. It was a long day, but we fitted in the Magical Mystery Tour, the Cavern Club, afternoon tea at The Beatles Story and an open-top bus tour.
What should you prioritise on a Liverpool day trip?
Book your main activity first, then build the rest of the day around it. Liverpool city centre is compact enough to fit a surprising amount in.
Is there gluten-free afternoon tea at The Beatles Story?
We had afternoon tea at The Beatles Story and gluten-free options were available, which made it an easy, relaxing break in a busy day.
Is Liverpool worth visiting?
Absolutely, whether you have one day or an entire weekend. Beyond The Beatles, Liverpool is bursting with character, architecture, music, culture and maritime history.
