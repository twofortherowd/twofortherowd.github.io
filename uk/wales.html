Common questions

Wales FAQ

How much time should I spend in Wales?
Cardiff is a good long weekend destination — enough time to explore the city and surrounding areas without rushing. For exploring the wider Welsh countryside and coast, give yourself at least a week to do it properly.
Is Cardiff worth visiting?
Absolutely. Cardiff is a brilliant capital city — compact enough to explore on foot, with excellent food and drink, good museums and galleries, and a genuine sense of place. It's one of the most underrated UK cities.
Do I need a car to explore Wales?
Cardiff and Swansea have good public transport and are very walkable. For exploring the wider Welsh countryside — Snowdonia, the Brecon Beacons, the Pembrokeshire Coast — having your own wheels gives you freedom and flexibility.
When is the best time to visit Wales?
Late spring through early autumn (May to September) offers the best weather. Coastal towns like Llandudno are lovely in summer and early autumn. Wales is beautiful year-round but winter can be rainy and grey.
