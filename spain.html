Your questions answered
Spain FAQ
Is Spain worth visiting?
Absolutely. Spain combines world-class architecture, beaches, food, culture and history with generally good value. The variety is extraordinary — Barcelona is nothing like Madrid, which is nothing like the coast.
Do Irish citizens need a visa for Spain?
No. Spain is part of the Schengen Area. Irish citizens can enter with just a passport and stay up to 90 days in any 180-day period. No visa or ESTA required.
How many days should I spend in Spain?
4–5 days minimum per city if you want to actually experience it. A week allows you to see Barcelona and Madrid properly. Two weeks allows Barcelona, Madrid and the coast.
Is the Sagrada Família worth it?
Yes. Book tickets online in advance to skip the queues. It is genuinely extraordinary — neither photos nor stories do it justice. Plan 1–2 hours inside.
What's the best time to visit Spain?
May–June or September–October. Summer is hot and crowded. Winter is mild on the coast but cooler in Madrid. Avoid August in cities — locals leave and prices spike.
Is it expensive?
Generally good value, especially outside major cities and tourist areas. Barcelona and Madrid are pricier. The coast offers better value. Spanish wine and food are excellent and affordable.
Do I need to tip in Spain?
Tipping is optional in Spain, unlike the USA. Rounding up or leaving 5–10% for good service is appreciated but not expected. At bars, leaving loose change is normal.
What's the food like?
Exceptional. Tapas culture, fresh seafood on the coast, Spanish wine, and incredible jamón ibérico. Dinner is typically 9pm onwards, and the meal is the social event — not just fuel.

Plan your Spain trip

Spain wants you to slow down. Skip the rushing between cities and spend real time in each one. Two weeks, if you can. This is not a place for ticking boxes.
