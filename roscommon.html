If you're a fan of Irish mythology, County Roscommon is going to be a real treat.

This is a county of legendary queens, ancient ceremonial landscapes and one of the most mysterious entrances to the Otherworld in Irish folklore. But it isn't all legends and spooky stories: there are magnificent lakes, forest parks, historic houses, medieval abbeys and fascinating mining heritage too.

It's another county that deserves far more attention than it gets, and once you start exploring, you'll see why.

Start here
Ancient Connacht & the Famine story
Royal landscape · Guided

👑 Rathcroghan

One of the most important royal landscapes in Ireland, with more than 240 archaeological sites linked to Queen Medb of Connacht. Start at the Rathcroghan Visitor Centre in Tulsk.

Why visit? Archaeology, mythology and Irish history.

🧭 Book a guided tour through the visitor centre to get the most out of it.
Otherworld · Guided only

🐈 Oweynagat, the Cave of the Cats

A narrow cave said to be a gateway to the Otherworld, linked to Samhain, the festival that became Halloween.

Why visit? Folklore and ancient mysteries.

⚠️ Access is restricted and can be dangerous. Only visit with an authorised guide.
Estate · Museum

🌾 Strokestown Park & National Famine Museum

A Georgian estate and an award-winning museum that tells the story of the Great Famine through the estate's own records.

Why visit? Irish history and heritage.

Castle · Free

🏰 Roscommon Castle

Impressive 13th-century castle ruins in Loughnaneane Park, beside the town.

Why visit? Castles and photography.

Windmill

🌬️ Elphin Windmill

A restored 18th-century windmill, a rare survivor in Ireland.

Why visit? Engineering and local heritage.

Folk museum

🏺 Derryglad Folk & Heritage Museum

A huge collection of objects from traditional Irish rural life, near Athlone.

Why visit? Social history.

03
Around the county
Boyle, Lough Key & the Shannon
Forest park · Family

🌲 Lough Key Forest & Activity Park

A lakeside forest park near Boyle with woodland walks, a tree-canopy walk, boating and activities for all ages.

Why visit? Families, nature and outdoor fun.

Abbey

⛪ Boyle Abbey

A remarkably well-preserved Cistercian abbey founded in the 12th century, with fine carved capitals.

Why visit? Medieval history.

Georgian house

🏛️ King House, Boyle

An elegant Georgian mansion with exhibitions on the history of the area.

Why visit? Historic houses and culture.

Mining heritage

⛏️ Arigna Mining Experience

Ex-miners guide you underground to tell the story of the coal mines that worked here until 1990.

Why visit? Industrial heritage and history.

River · Lake

🌊 The Shannon & Lough Ree

Boating, nature and beautiful scenery along the county's eastern edge.

Why visit? Water activities and relaxation.

Walking route

🥾 Suck Valley Way

A long-distance walk through quiet countryside and villages in the west of the county.

Why visit? Walking and countryside.

History, myth & mystery
Myths, legends & history

History and legend, clearly labelled.

Legend

👑 Queen Medb of Connacht

Medb was the fierce queen at the heart of the Táin Bó Cúailnge, the great cattle raid in which she set out to steal the Brown Bull of Cooley. Rathcroghan is said to be where her story began.

Folklore

🐈 Oweynagat and Samhain

At Samhain, the boundary between worlds was thought to open, and creatures poured out of Oweynagat: fierce cats, red birds and the Morrígan herself.

History

🌾 The Great Famine

Strokestown Park's archive holds letters and records from the Famine years, including the evictions and the 'coffin ships'. The museum tells this story powerfully.

History

🛡️ The kings of Connacht

The O'Conors, kings of Connacht, ruled from Roscommon, and two of them became High Kings of Ireland.

Common questions
County Roscommon FAQ
Can you go into the Cave of the Cats?
Only with an authorised guide. It's narrow, muddy and potentially dangerous, so book through the Rathcroghan Visitor Centre.
Is Roscommon good for families?
Yes. Lough Key Forest and Activity Park is a brilliant family day out, and the Arigna Mining Experience suits older children.
How long do you need at Strokestown Park?
Allow at least half a day for the museum, house and grounds.
