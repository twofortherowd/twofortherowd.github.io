Why we keep coming back
Our Kilkenny

Kilkenny isn't a county we visited once and ticked off — it's one we go back to regularly. The medieval city itself is a big part of that: narrow laneways, Kilkenny Castle sitting over the river, and a nightlife scene that genuinely holds its own against much bigger Irish cities.

We've been to Kilkenny's Christmas markets more than once, and if you're gluten-free like Nicola, the crêpes there deserve a special mention — some of the best we've had anywhere. Beyond the city, we've found our way out to Kilfane Glen and its waterfall near Thomastown, and to Shankill Castle, a family-run house right on the Kilkenny-Carlow border. We've also called into Mountain View in Ballyhale, which runs a proper weekend farmers market worth timing a visit around.

Good history, good food, good nightlife — Kilkenny keeps pulling us back.

Where we start every trip
Kilkenny City
The big one

🏰 Kilkenny Castle

Sitting right over the River Nore, Kilkenny Castle is the city's anchor point and its most recognisable landmark. Formal gardens and parkland make it an easy stop whether you've an hour or an afternoon.

Walk the mile

🚶 Medieval Mile Museum

Housed in the former St Mary's Church, the Medieval Mile Museum is a good anchor for the self-guided trail through Kilkenny's medieval past that runs between the castle and the cathedral.

Climb the tower

⛪ St Canice's Cathedral & Round Tower

Kilkenny's cathedral comes with its own round tower, which you can climb for views out over the city and beyond. One of the more active things to do in the city centre.

16th-century merchant house

🏛️ Rothe House & Garden

A beautifully preserved 16th-century merchant's house with restored gardens behind it — a proper look at how well-off Kilkenny families actually lived, not just a facade.

🎄 The Christmas markets

We've made it to Kilkenny's Christmas markets more than once, and they're a lovely way to see the medieval streets dressed up for the season. If you're gluten-free, seek out the crêpes — genuinely some of the best GF options we've come across at any Irish market.

Beyond the city walls
Around the county
A hidden garden

💦 Kilfane Glen & Waterfall

A restored 1790s romantic garden near Thomastown, with woodland paths, a grotto and a genuine waterfall tucked into the glen. Quieter and more atmospheric than most of Kilkenny's better-known attractions.

Watch our Kilfane video ↓
On the Carlow border

🏯 Shankill Castle

A family-run castle and gardens near Paulstown, right on the Kilkenny-Carlow border. Guided tours are led personally by a member of the family who lives there, which gives it a completely different feel to a standard heritage-site visit.

Watch our Shankill Castle video ↓
Cistercian ruin

⛪ Jerpoint Abbey

An outstanding 12th-century Cistercian abbey ruin near Thomastown, easily paired with Kilfane Glen for one countryside day out from the city.

Time it for the weekend

🛒 Mountain View, Ballyhale

A family-run venue in Ballyhale that also hosts a proper farmers market at weekends — worth timing your visit around if you're in the area on a Saturday or Sunday.

💡 Our local tip

Visit Kilkenny Castle and the Medieval Mile in the morning, then cross the river for lunch and a stroll along the Butterslip. Stay for dinner and live music — that's when Kilkenny really comes alive.

🪨 Did you know?

Brownshill Dolmen — just outside our own home county of Carlow, but well worth a visit from Kilkenny — has one of the heaviest capstones in Europe, estimated at around 100 tonnes.

Common questions
County Kilkenny FAQ
Is Kilkenny worth visiting?
Absolutely — we go back regularly ourselves. Between Kilkenny Castle, the Medieval Mile, the Christmas markets and the nightlife, it's one of the Irish cities we keep returning to.
What are Kilkenny's Christmas markets like?
We've visited Kilkenny's Christmas markets ourselves and can vouch for the gluten-free crêpes in particular. It's a lovely way to see the medieval city dressed up for the season.
Can you visit Shankill Castle?
Yes. Shankill Castle, near Paulstown on the Kilkenny/Carlow border, offers guided tours of the house along with gardens and a café. We've visited and filmed there ourselves.
What's a good day trip from Kilkenny city?
Jerpoint Abbey and Kilfane Glen & Waterfall, both near Thomastown, make an easy pairing. Our printable Kilkenny guide also covers Inistioge, Castlecomer Discovery Park and Graiguenamanagh as easy day trips.
Do you have a Kilkenny guide I can use?
Yes — we've put together our own printable Kilkenny local guide covering attractions, places to eat, coffee stops, pubs, shopping, hidden gems and easy day trips. You'll find it further up this page.
