Fuengirola —
A Beach Town We Keep Returning To

We've visited Fuengirola three times now. Each time we come back, we stay a little longer. The beach is beautiful, the weather is perfect, and there are people we keep going back to see. The most recent visit was in 2024 with Padraig, visiting friends in the area. Fuengirola has become the kind of place that doesn't need much justification — we just want to be there.

✈️ Why We Return

The kind of place that keeps you coming back

«By the third visit, Fuengirola wasn't a destination anymore. It was just a place we wanted to be.»

Fuengirola is not dramatic. It's not famous like Barcelona. It's not culturally important like Madrid. But it has something that matters more — it has the kind of atmosphere and people that make you want to stay. The first visit was a surprise. The second time we came back because we'd enjoyed it. By the third time, it was simply a place we wanted to be.

The beach is genuinely beautiful. The weather is reliably good. And the seafront is the kind of place where you sit with a beer and watch life happen. St Patrick's Bar's karaoke nights became a running joke — Padraig singing the Cranberries on a warm Spanish evening became one of those travel moments you don't forget.

🎤 The Karaoke Story: St Patrick's Bar karaoke on the seafront remains one of the best travel memories. Padraig, slightly emboldened by Spanish beer and good company, singing the Cranberries. The moment your travel companions become comfortable enough to do ridiculous things is the moment you know you've found the right place.
🏖️ Fuengirola Attractions
🏖️
The Beach

Long, sandy, golden. Perfect for swimming, sunbathing, or just sitting and watching the Mediterranean. The real heart of Fuengirola.

☕
Seafront Cafés

Beer, coffee, tapas, and time to think. The seafront café culture is the best part of Fuengirola.

🍽️
Fresh Seafood

Being on the Mediterranean means excellent fish restaurants. The seafood is fresh and the prices are reasonable.

🎵
St Patrick's Bar

Irish bar on the seafront with karaoke nights and a good mix of locals and travelers. Where memories are made.

🛕
Fuengirola Castle

18th century castle with views across the coast. Worth the visit if you want something beyond the beach.

🚶
Coastal Walks

The coastline is beautiful for walking — head east or west along the beach and you'll find quieter spots.
