Leitrim might be one of Ireland's least-populated counties, but don't mistake that for having nothing to do. Quite the opposite!

With beautiful lakes, peaceful waterways, forest trails, historic castles and wonderfully atmospheric countryside, it's perfect for getting away from the crowds. It's home to one of Ireland's most famous waterfalls, pretty waterside towns and traditional villages.

And did you know Leitrim has an Atlantic coastline? It's only a few kilometres long, but it definitely counts!

🧭 A quick note from Nicola and Podge: this is a researched guide rather than a diary of our own visits. When we explore and film in Leitrim, our own videos and first-hand tips will be added here, just like on our other county guides.

🛂 Near the border

North Leitrim is a short drive from Sligo, and the south of the county borders Fermanagh. Cross into Fermanagh and prices change to pounds sterling (£) and speed limits to miles per hour.

North Leitrim: waterfalls & castles
Waterfall · Yeats

💧 Glencar Waterfall

A waterfall tumbling through woodland near Glencar Lough, mentioned in Yeats's poem The Stolen Child. It's at its best after rain.

Why visit? Nature, photography and families.

Castle · Seasonal

🏰 Parke's Castle

A restored 17th-century plantation castle on the shore of Lough Gill, with guided tours.

Why visit? History and architecture.

📅 Open seasonally. Check Heritage Ireland before you go.
Friary ruins

⛪ Creevelea Abbey

Atmospheric Franciscan friary ruins near Dromahair, founded in 1508, surrounded by peaceful countryside.

Why visit? Medieval history and photography.

Castle ruins

🏰 Manorhamilton Castle

The remains of a 17th-century castle built by Sir Frederick Hamilton.

Why visit? Local history.

Gardens

🥕 The Organic Centre, Rossinver

A centre for organic gardening and sustainability, with gardens and courses.

Why visit? Gardening and nature.

Coast

🌊 Tullaghan & the Leitrim coast

Leitrim's tiny stretch of Atlantic coast, with views across Donegal Bay.

Why visit? Coastal scenery and bragging rights.

Around the county
South Leitrim: the Shannon & the lakes
Town · Boating

⛵ Carrick-on-Shannon

A lively Shannon town, famous for boating and riverside atmosphere. Don't miss the Costello Memorial Chapel, often described as one of the smallest chapels in the world.

Why visit? Weekend breaks, boating and food.

Lake

🌊 Lough Allen

A big, scenic lake surrounded by hills, the first lake on the Shannon.

Why visit? Nature, boating and photography.

Boardwalk

🪵 Acres Lake Boardwalk, Drumshanbo

A boardwalk that floats out over the lake, part of the Shannon Blueway.

Why visit? Easy walks and photos.

Blueway

🛶 The Shannon Blueway

Walking, cycling and paddling routes along the Shannon waterways.

Why visit? Outdoor recreation and family adventures.

🌊 Use reputable providers for kayaking and boat trips.
Distillery

🥃 The Shed Distillery, Drumshanbo

Home of Drumshanbo Gunpowder Irish Gin, with visitor experiences on the distilling story.

Why visit? Adults interested in distilling.

Railway heritage

🚂 Cavan & Leitrim Railway, Dromod

A volunteer-run heritage project on the old narrow-gauge line.

Why visit? Transport history.

ℹ️ Check current opening arrangements before you go.

History, myth & mystery
Myths, legends & history

History and legend, clearly labelled.

Legend

🐉 The Dobhar-chú of Glenade

The 'water hound' is said to be a giant otter-like creature. In 1722 a local woman, Grace Connolly, was reportedly killed by one at Glenade Lough, and her gravestone in Conwal cemetery is said to bear its carving.

Literature

💧 Yeats and Glencar

"Where the wandering water gushes / From the hills above Glen-Car" – Yeats wrote Glencar into The Stolen Child, his poem about fairies luring a child away.

History

👑 The Kingdom of Breifne

Much of Leitrim lay in West Breifne, ruled by the O'Rourkes. Their stronghold stood near Dromahair, where Parke's Castle was later built.

Legend

🌊 Sionann and the Shannon

The goddess Sionann was swept away by the waters of a magical well, creating the Shannon. The river's traditional source, the Shannon Pot, is just over the border in Cavan.

Common questions
County Leitrim FAQ
Is Glencar Waterfall in Sligo or Leitrim?
Leitrim. It's very close to the Sligo border, so a lot of Sligo-based trips include it.
Does Leitrim really have a coastline?
Yes, a few kilometres of Atlantic coast around Tullaghan, between Sligo and Donegal.
How many days do you need in Leitrim?
Two or three days to see the north and the Shannon country properly.
Have Two for the Row'd visited these places?
