Night one — and already winning (literally)

📓 Nicola's diary

Check-in and bag drop was seamless — literally onboard and through passport control within 15 minutes of arriving at the port. The airport authorities could learn a lesson or two from these cruise ship crews!

There's absolutely loads to do onboard — trivia, pub quizzes, spa treatments, cocktails... and that's just before lunch! We dipped our toes into a couple of the quizzes and, would you believe it, we actually won one!

The cocktail menu was thoroughly explored — purely for research purposes, of course. Dinner at Murano's was DIVINE, and we also tried our luck in the casino, which basically means we donated a few dollars to the ship's retirement fund. 😂

Alaska cruise FAQ

What was our Alaska cruise itinerary?
Our 8-day round-trip cruise sailed from Vancouver through the Inside Passage to Icy Strait Point, Hubbard Glacier, Juneau and Ketchikan before returning to Vancouver.
What were our favourite Alaska cruise experiences?
Dog sledding in Juneau, bear watching from Icy Strait Point, Hubbard Glacier scenic cruising and the Ketchikan Lumberjack Show were our standout experiences.
Did we need a shore excursion at Hubbard Glacier?
No. Hubbard Glacier was a scenic cruising day viewed from the ship rather than a port where we went ashore.
