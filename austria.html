Austria —
Mozart, mountains and classical music

Nicola first visited Austria on an interrail trip in 2007 and returned in 2010. Vienna's imperial grandeur and world-class concert halls, the Mozart-saturated beauty of Salzburg, the spectacularly Alpine Innsbruck and the quietly transformed city of Linz make Austria one of the most rewarding countries in Europe.

🇦🇹 Austria at a glance

Quick facts before you go

A few useful things to know before you start plotting the route.

🏙️ Capital
Vienna — imperial, musical and a very good place to start an Austria trip.
🗣️ Language
German. English is widely useful in the main visitor areas, but a few German phrases never hurt.
💶 Currency
Euro — one less thing to think about if you're travelling from Ireland.
🕐 Time
Central European Time — normally one hour ahead of Ireland.
🔌 Plugs
European two-pin plugs, so pack an adaptor if you're coming from Ireland.
🚆 Getting around
Austria is made for rail travel. Vienna, Linz, Salzburg and Innsbruck fit beautifully into a multi-city train trip.

❤️ Why Austria?

Four cities — and four very different versions of Austria

Austria packs an enormous amount into a relatively compact country. You can spend one day surrounded by imperial architecture and classical music in Vienna, another following Mozart through Salzburg, and then find yourself looking straight up at the Alps from the streets of Innsbruck.

Linz adds something different again: a Danube city where historic streets sit alongside art, technology and a much more modern side of Austria.

Nicola first travelled through Austria by Interrail in 2007 and returned in 2010. That is still how we'd think about planning it: don't automatically stop at Vienna. Austria's rail network makes combining cities remarkably straightforward, and the journey west towards Salzburg and Innsbruck is part of the fun.

🍰 What to eat in Austria

Come hungry

Austria does comfort food and cake extremely well. These are a few names worth watching for as you travel around the country.

🍽️ Wiener Schnitzel
The Viennese classic: thin, breaded and fried — simple, crisp and very satisfying.
🍫 Sachertorte
Vienna's famous chocolate cake, traditionally served with unsweetened whipped cream.
🍎 Apfelstrudel
Thin pastry wrapped around spiced apple filling — one of the Austrian classics.
🥞 Kaiserschmarrn
Fluffy shredded pancake, usually dusted with sugar and served with fruit compote.
🏔️ Salzburger Nockerl
A light baked soufflé-style Salzburg speciality shaped to echo the city's surrounding peaks.
🍰 Linzer Torte
The lattice-topped nut pastry associated with Linz — save room for a slice.

❓ FAQ
Is Vienna worth visiting?
Vienna is one of the great cities of the world. Imperial architecture, world-class museums, the finest classical music tradition and a historic coffee-house culture recognised in Austria's national inventory of intangible cultural heritage. A classical concert in Vienna is a travel experience that stays with you forever.
Is Salzburg worth visiting?
Absolutely. Salzburg is one of the most beautiful small cities in Europe. Mozart was born here and his presence is felt everywhere. The old town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Hohensalzburg Fortress is magnificent and the mountain backdrop is extraordinary.
Is Innsbruck worth visiting?
Yes. Innsbruck is one of those cities that genuinely stops you in your tracks. The combination of a beautiful medieval old town and the dramatic Alpine peaks rising directly behind it is unlike anything else in Europe. Compact, walkable and spectacularly beautiful.
Is Austria good for interrailing?
Austria is one of the best interrailing countries in Europe. The rail network is excellent, the cities are varied and rewarding, and the country is small enough to cover a lot of ground quickly. Nicola interrailed through Vienna, Salzburg, Innsbruck and Linz in 2007 and it remains one of her favourite travel experiences.

Ready for Austria?

Start with Vienna, add Salzburg, stop in Innsbruck and don't overlook Linz. Book a classical concert, walk the Mozart trail and take the cable car above the Alps.
