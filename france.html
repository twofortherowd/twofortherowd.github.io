🇫🇷 Why We Keep Going Back

Twenty years — and France still doesn't feel “done”

“Every region seems to have its own food, architecture, landscape and personality. That's exactly why we keep returning.”

France has been part of our travels since 2006. We've done the famous sights, but some of our strongest memories are the smaller ones: driving through Brittany with no fixed plan, standing quietly on the Normandy beaches, wandering through villages in Alsace, seeing Provence in bloom and stopping for food in Lyon simply because somewhere looked good.

Being able to speak French has also changed the way we travel here. It makes the small conversations easier, gives us more confidence away from the obvious tourist trail and has helped turn France into somewhere we feel genuinely comfortable returning to.

Common questions

France FAQ

Where have you visited in France?
Our trips have taken us through Brittany, Normandy, Paris, the Loire Valley, Dordogne, Vichy, Clermont-Ferrand, Lyon, Lille, Bordeaux, Provence, the Côte d'Azur, Alsace and Lourdes, as well as Disneyland Paris. Use the destination cards above for the full individual guides.
Is France good for a road trip?
Very much so. Some of our favourite France experiences came from having the freedom to stop in smaller towns and villages between the headline destinations. The best region depends on whether you want coast, history, wine country, mountains or cities.
Which guide should I start with?
Start with the destination you're actually considering rather than trying to read France as one enormous itinerary. That's why we've split this page into individual guides.
Do you have a separate Disneyland Paris guide?
Yes. Disneyland Paris now has its own page rather than being buried inside the general France or Paris content.
