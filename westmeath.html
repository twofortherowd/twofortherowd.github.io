County Westmeath sits right in the heart of Ireland, and there's a lot more to it than motorways and handy coffee stops!

Known as the Lake County, it has beautiful waterways, historic towns, atmospheric monasteries and great cultural attractions. It's also home to the Hill of Uisneach, traditionally the mythological centre of Ireland.

Add lively Athlone and Mullingar, impressive estates and plenty of peaceful countryside, and you've got a brilliant short-break destination.

🧭 A quick note from Nicola and Podge: this is a researched guide rather than a diary of our own visits. When we explore and film in Westmeath, our own videos and first-hand tips will be added here, just like on our other county guides.

Uisneach, Belvedere & the north
Sacred hill · Booked tours

🔥 The Hill of Uisneach

One of Ireland's most important ancient ceremonial landscapes, linked with kingship, sacred fires and the goddess Ériu. Tradition calls it the centre of Ireland.

Why visit? Mythology, archaeology and history.

🎟️ The hill is privately owned. Visits are by guided tour, booked in advance.
Estate · Gardens

🧱 Belvedere House, Gardens & Park

An 18th-century house on Lough Ennell with gardens, woodland trails and the famous Jealous Wall, the biggest folly in Ireland.

Why visit? Families, gardens and history.

Abbey · Folklore

✝️ Fore Abbey

The ruins of a Benedictine priory in a peaceful valley near Castlepollard, surrounded by the 'Seven Wonders of Fore'.

Why visit? Medieval history and folklore.

Castle · Gardens

🏰 Tullynally Castle Gardens

The gardens of the Pakenham family's Gothic castle near Castlepollard.

Why visit? Gardens and photography.

📅 Seasonal opening. Check before you go.
Lakes

🌊 Lough Owel & Lough Ennell

Two beautiful lakes either side of Mullingar, for walks, fishing and peaceful views.

Why visit? Nature and relaxation.

Lake · Legend

🦢 Lough Derravaragh

The lake where, legend says, the Children of Lir spent their first 300 years as swans.

Why visit? Legends and lake views.

Around the county
Athlone, Mullingar & the south
Castle · Reopening

🏰 Athlone Castle

A castle on the Shannon at the heart of Athlone's story, including the 1691 siege.

Why visit? Castles and Irish history.

🚧 The visitor centre is closed for redevelopment, with reopening expected in autumn 2026. Check before you go.
Pub · Record

🍺 Sean's Bar, Athlone

Said to be the oldest pub in Ireland, with a history going back to around 900 AD. There's sawdust on the floor and music most nights.

Why visit? A pint with a story.

Distillery

🥃 Kilbeggan Distillery Experience

A distillery licensed since 1757, with tours of its old waterwheel-powered buildings.

Why visit? Industrial history and adult visitors.

Heritage park

🌿 Dún na Sí, Moate

A heritage park exploring Irish culture, folklore and rural traditions.

Why visit? Families and heritage.

Greenway

🚲 Royal Canal Greenway

Flat, family-friendly walking and cycling along the canal through Mullingar.

Why visit? Cycling, walking and families.

County town

🎶 Mullingar

A lively town for music, shopping and easy access to the lakes.

Why visit? Town exploring and food.

History, myth & mystery
Myths, legends & history

History and legend, clearly labelled.

Myth

🔥 The Hill of Uisneach

Legend says the first fire in Ireland was lit at Uisneach, and Bealtaine fires were lit here to mark the start of summer. The Catstone, a huge boulder, is said to mark the place where the provinces met, and the goddess Ériu, who gave Ireland its name, is said to rest beneath it.

Folklore

✝️ The Seven Wonders of Fore

They include the monastery built on a bog, the water that flows uphill, the tree with three branches that won't burn, and the water that won't boil. Each has its own story, and you can find them all on a walk around the valley.

History

🧱 The Jealous Wall

In the 1760s Robert Rochfort built a huge fake ruin at Belvedere to block his view of his brother's grander house next door. He also kept his wife locked up at another estate for more than 30 years after accusing her of an affair with another brother.

Legend

🦢 The Children of Lir

Jealous Aoife turned her four stepchildren into swans, condemning them to 300 years on Lough Derravaragh, 300 on the Sea of Moyle and 300 off the Mayo coast.

History

🏰 The Siege of Athlone, 1691

Athlone's bridge over the Shannon made it a vital crossing in the Williamite War. In 1691 Williamite forces stormed across the river and took the town.

Before you go
Practical travel tips
🚆 Getting around
Athlone and Mullingar have rail and bus links. A car helps for rural sites.
🗓️ Best time
April to October.
⏳ How long
Two or three days.
💶 Currency
Euro.
🎟️ Booking
Book Uisneach tours in advance.
🗺️ County lines
Athlone straddles Westmeath and Roscommon. The castle is on the Westmeath bank. Ballinasloe is in Galway.

Opening times, tours and access arrangements change, so please check official websites before you travel. Useful links: Discover Ireland and Athlone Castle.

Common questions
County Westmeath FAQ
Is Athlone Castle open?
The visitor centre closed in October 2025 for redevelopment, with reopening expected in autumn 2026, subject to confirmation. Check athlonecastle.ie before you go.
Can you just walk up the Hill of Uisneach?
No. It's private land, and visits are by guided tour booked in advance.
Is Sean's Bar really Ireland's oldest pub?
It claims a history back to around 900 AD and is widely called Ireland's oldest pub. Either way, it's a great spot for a pint and a tune.
Have Two for the Row'd visited these places?
