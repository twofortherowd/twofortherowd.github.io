🇮🇱 Israel Christian pilgrimage · 2018 9 days

Our Holy Land journey —
walking through the stories of the Gospels

Nicola travelled through the Holy Land on a nine-day Christian pilgrimage taking in Jerusalem, Bethlehem, the Sea of Galilee, Nazareth, Masada, the Dead Sea and the Mediterranean coast. It remains one of the most moving and extraordinary trips she has ever taken.

⚠️ Travel note: This guide describes our experience in 2018. Conditions have changed significantly. Always check the Irish DFA’s current travel advice before considering travel to Israel or the occupied Palestinian territory.

Common questions
Israel and Holy Land FAQ
Was nine days enough?

It covered an extraordinary amount, but the pace was busy. A guided itinerary made it possible to connect the major Christian sites, desert landscapes and coastal cities in one journey.

What was the highlight?

The Sea of Galilee and Nicola’s Jordan River baptism were the most personal and moving experiences of the trip.

Can Bethlehem be visited from Jerusalem?

Bethlehem is in the occupied West Bank. Our 2018 tour crossed from Jerusalem and changed to a Palestinian guide for the visit. Current access and travel advice must be checked.

Is the Dead Sea worth visiting?

Yes. Floating is genuinely unforgettable, and combining it with Masada makes an excellent day.

Is it currently safe to travel?

This account reflects 2018, not present conditions. Check the Irish DFA and relevant official guidance before making any arrangements.
