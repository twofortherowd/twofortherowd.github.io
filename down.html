Magical Mountains, Coastal Castles & Seaside Escapes

Where the Mountains of Mourne sweep down towards the sea and extraordinary adventures are never far away.

County Down is one of those places that seems determined to show off!

You've got the magnificent Mourne Mountains, lovely seaside towns, ancient castles, grand estates and some of the prettiest coastal scenery in Ireland. It's steeped in stories too, from St Patrick's final resting place to the legends of the Mournes, and Game of Thrones fans will spot plenty of familiar locations.

Whether you're climbing mountains, exploring Strangford Lough or eating fish and chips by the sea in Newcastle, County Down has something for everyone.

🧭 A quick note from Nicola and Podge: this is a researched guide rather than a diary of our own visits. When we explore and film in Down, our own videos and first-hand tips will be added here, just like on our other county guides.

🛂 Crossing from the Republic?

There are no border checks, but prices are in pounds sterling (£), speed limits are in miles per hour, and it's worth checking your phone plan and car insurance cover before you go.

Start here
The Mournes, Newcastle & St Patrick's country
Mountains · Hiking

⛰️ The Mourne Mountains

Granite peaks, reservoirs and valleys dominate the south of the county. Slieve Donard (850m) is the highest mountain in Northern Ireland, and the dry-stone Mourne Wall links fifteen summits.

Why visit? Hiking, photography and mountain scenery.

⛰️ Mountain weather changes fast. Pick routes that match your experience, and carry layers, food and a map.
Seaside town

🏖️ Newcastle

Where the Mountains of Mourne sweep down to the sea, Newcastle has a long promenade, a beach, plenty of places to eat and a great base for the mountains.

Why visit? Families, seaside walks and short breaks.

Forest park · Film location

🌲 Tollymore Forest Park

Woodland, the Shimna River, stone bridges and follies. Several Game of Thrones scenes were filmed here.

Why visit? Nature, photography, families and film fans.

Cathedral · St Patrick

✝️ Downpatrick & Down Cathedral

Tradition says St Patrick is buried in the grounds of Down Cathedral, under a huge granite stone, and that Saints Brigid and Columba lie with him. The St Patrick Centre in town tells his story.

Why visit? Early Christian heritage and Irish history.

Forest park · Family

🌀 Castlewellan Forest Park

A lake, woodland trails, an arboretum and the famous Peace Maze, one of the largest permanent hedge mazes in the world.

Why visit? Families, walking and cycling.

Reservoir · Walks

💧 Silent Valley

A reservoir deep in the Mournes, with designated walks, dramatic scenery and the story of the engineering that supplies Belfast's water.

Why visit? Landscapes and engineering heritage.

Around the county
Strangford Lough, estates & the north coast
National Trust · Film location

🏰 Castle Ward

An 18th-century house with one Classical front and one Gothic front, because the owners couldn't agree. The farmyard became Winterfell in Game of Thrones.

Why visit? Historic houses, nature and film fans.

National Trust · Gardens

🌺 Mount Stewart

On the Ards Peninsula, with some of the finest gardens in the British Isles, a grand house and lakeside walks.

Why visit? Gardens, heritage and relaxing walks.

Sea lough · Wildlife

🦆 Strangford Lough

An internationally important wildlife area, ringed by villages and viewpoints. A small car ferry links Strangford and Portaferry across the narrow mouth of the lough.

Why visit? Wildlife, scenery and peaceful coastal exploring.

⛴️ Check the Strangford–Portaferry ferry timetable before you plan around it.
Royal residence · Seasonal

👑 Hillsborough Castle & Gardens

The official royal residence in Northern Ireland, with gardens and guided tours on its public visiting days.

Why visit? Architecture, history and gardens.

📅 Check the official visitor schedule. It isn't open every day.
Coast · Family

⛵ Bangor & the North Down coast

A marina town with coastal walks to Crawfordsburn Country Park and Helen's Bay, where woodland meets the shore.

Why visit? Seaside days and family outings.

History, myth & mystery
Myths, legends & history

History and legend, clearly labelled.

Tradition

✝️ St Patrick in Downpatrick

Tradition holds that Patrick landed near Saul, founded his first church there and died there around 461. Down Cathedral marks his traditional grave, and Brigid and Columba are said to be buried with him.

History

🧱 The Mourne Wall

Built between 1904 and 1922 to enclose the catchment of the Silent Valley reservoir, the wall runs for about 22 miles over fifteen mountains. Walking its length is a classic Mourne challenge.

Legend

⭕ The Giant's Ring

Built around 2700 BC, the Giant's Ring has drawn stories for centuries. In the 1700s it was even used as a horse-racing track!

Film

🐉 Westeros in County Down

Castle Ward's farmyard became Winterfell, and Tollymore's forests and bridges appear in the opening episodes of Game of Thrones.

Before you go
Practical travel tips
🚗 Getting around
A car helps for rural spots. Bangor and Newcastle have public transport links.
🗓️ Best time
May to September for walking and coastal exploring.
⏳ How long
Three or four days.
💷 Currency
Pounds sterling. Speed limits are in miles per hour.
⛰️ Safety
The Mournes need proper mountain preparation. Take care on beaches and tidal areas.
🗺️ County lines
Belfast straddles Antrim and Down, with the River Lagan roughly marking the line.

Opening times, tours and access arrangements change, so please check official websites before you travel. Useful links: Discover Northern Ireland and National Trust.

Common questions
County Down FAQ
How high is Slieve Donard?
850 metres, making it the highest mountain in Northern Ireland. It's a serious hill walk, so check the weather and go prepared.
Where is St Patrick buried?
Tradition places his grave in the grounds of Down Cathedral in Downpatrick, marked by a large granite stone.
Can I visit the Game of Thrones locations?
Yes. Castle Ward and Tollymore Forest Park are both open to visitors. Check the National Trust and forest park arrangements before you go.
What currency is used in County Down?
Pounds sterling (£). There are no border checks if you're coming from the Republic.
Have Two for the Row'd visited these places?
