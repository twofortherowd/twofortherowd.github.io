Common questions

Travel gift FAQ

What is a good Christmas gift for someone who loves to travel?

Practical things they’ll use on every trip make the best travel gifts — packing cubes, an AirTag for their luggage, a universal adapter or a good travel pillow. For something more memorable, give an experience: a day tour, food tour or attraction ticket for their next trip.

What are good stocking fillers for travellers?

Small things that solve real travel annoyances: ear plugs, an eye mask, a luggage scale, compression socks or an Irish passport wallet. They’re inexpensive but get used on every single trip.

Can I give a tour or experience as a Christmas present?

Yes — booking a day trip, food tour or attraction ticket for their next holiday makes a lovely gift. Many activities on GetYourGuide have free cancellation, which helps if travel dates aren’t fixed yet; check each listing’s terms before you book.
