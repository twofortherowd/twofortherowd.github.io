Don’t get caught out

How to bag a genuine Black Friday travel bargain

  1. Shortlist now. Save the tours, tickets and hotels you’re interested in this week, so you can act quickly when the sales go live.
  2. Note today’s price. A quick screenshot means you’ll know whether the “deal” is really cheaper.
  3. Compare what’s included, not just the price. Group size, meals, transport and the operator’s reviews all matter.
  4. Check the cancellation terms. Flexible bookings let you grab a deal now without being locked in.
  5. Sign in before you pay. Loyalty savings like TourRadar+ only apply when you’re logged in.
  6. Codes vs sale prices. Promo codes often can’t be combined with sale prices — work out which gives the bigger saving.

Common questions

Black Friday travel FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 is on Friday 27 November, and Cyber Monday is on Monday 30 November. Many travel sales start earlier in the week and run through the weekend.

Is Black Friday a good time to book tours and travel?

It can be. Travel companies typically use Black Friday to fill trips for the year ahead, so it’s a good moment to book something you were planning anyway. The trick is to shortlist and price-check now, so you can tell a genuine deal from a dressed-up full price.

Does TourRadar have Black Friday deals?

TourRadar typically runs Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals, with tour operators cutting prices on trips for the year ahead. Your free TourRadar+ saving is also applied automatically at checkout when you’re signed in. See our TourRadar discount guide.

Can I use a Klook promo code on Black Friday sale prices?

Usually not. Klook’s promo code terms say codes generally can’t be combined with already-discounted items or other promotions, so compare the sale price with the full price plus a code and pick whichever works out cheaper. Our Klook codes page lists the codes that are in date right now.
