Bethlehem —more beautiful and more alive than we ever expected

Our visit to Bethlehem was one of the most interesting and thought-provoking parts of Nicola's 2018 Holy Land pilgrimage: the Church of the Nativity, Manger Square, the separation wall and a Palestinian guide whose knowledge of the biblical sites was extraordinary.

Important context

This page describes our 2018 visit

Travel note: The situation in Israel and the Palestinian territories has changed significantly since our visit. This page is a first-hand travel story from 2018, not current security advice. Always check the Irish Department of Foreign Affairs and other official guidance before considering travel.

Our experience

Crossing into Bethlehem

“Two guides, two perspectives on the same land — both knowledgeable, both proud of their connection to these places.”

Bethlehem is in the West Bank, and crossing from Israel into Palestinian territory was an experience in itself. At the checkpoint, the Jewish Israeli guide who had accompanied the group for most of the trip was replaced by a Muslim Palestinian guide who took over for the visit.

That change of guide made the complexity of the region feel immediate rather than abstract. The Palestinian guide's knowledge of the biblical sites in Bethlehem was extraordinary, and the visit became one of the most memorable parts of the wider pilgrimage.
