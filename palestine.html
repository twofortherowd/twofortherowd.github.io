Palestine & the West Bank —far more than Bethlehem

Our 2018 Holy Land journey took us beyond Bethlehem into the wider West Bank and Jordan Valley, including Jericho and the Jordan River area. What stayed with us most was the contrast: ancient pilgrimage sites, busy Palestinian cities, stark checkpoints, desert landscapes and the experience of hearing different guides explain the same land from very different perspectives.

Bethlehem

Crossing into the West Bank

“Two guides, two perspectives on the same land — both knowledgeable, both deeply connected to these places.”

Bethlehem was where the geography and politics of the region stopped feeling abstract. At the checkpoint, the Israeli guide who had accompanied the group was replaced by a Palestinian guide for the Bethlehem visit.

We visited the Church of the Nativity and Manger Square, and we also saw the separation barrier and Palestinian street art. The guide's knowledge of the biblical sites was extraordinary, but the surrounding city mattered just as much: this was not simply a pilgrimage stop, but a living Palestinian city.

Jericho

Ancient Jericho and the Jordan Valley

Our route also took us through Jericho and the Jordan Valley. Ancient Jericho, or Tell es-Sultan, sits northwest of the modern city and preserves archaeological layers stretching back thousands of years.

The landscape around Jericho could hardly have felt more different from Jerusalem: dry, low-lying and dramatic, with bare mountains rising from the valley. It is one of those places where geography and history are inseparable.

Jordan River

Qasr al-Yahud and the baptism route

Qasr al-Yahud on the Jordan River was another major stop on our pilgrimage. For Nicola, the Jordan River experience became one of the most personal moments of the entire journey.

The route through this part of the West Bank also made the distances and borders feel very real: checkpoints, controlled crossings and desert roads were part of the journey, not just something we had read about beforehand.

Beyond our own stops

Other Palestinian cities worth knowing about

Our own route focused on Bethlehem, Jericho and the Jordan Valley, but Palestine is much broader than the places we personally visited. Other major destinations include Ramallah, Nablus and Hebron / Al-Khalil. We are not presenting those as first-hand recommendations — they are simply useful places to research when planning a wider visit.
