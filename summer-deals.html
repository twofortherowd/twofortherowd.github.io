☀️ Autumn Sun — Where the Warmth Still Is

Long after the rest of Europe cools down, these destinations keep delivering — the Canaries and Madeira stay warm right through winter, Malta and Cyprus stretch the Med season into October, and North Africa and the Middle East finally become comfortable to explore properly.

🇪🇸
🎃 Perfect for October mid-term

Gran Canaria

Reliable 25°C+ temperatures well into November, spectacular volcanic landscapes, sand dunes and family-friendly resorts. One of Ireland's most-booked destinations for late-autumn sun.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family favourite

Tenerife

Ireland's favourite Canary Island — the biggest, warmest and most varied. Mount Teide, Loro Parque, black-sand beaches and dependable winter sun. Direct flights from Dublin, Cork and Shannon.

🌋 Volcanic beauty

Lanzarote

The most striking of the Canaries — Timanfaya's lunar volcanic landscapes, César Manrique's architecture and calm resort towns. Less crowded than Tenerife but every bit as warm through autumn.

📈 Something different

Island-hop across the Canaries by sailboat — see multiple islands in one trip, snorkel in calm bays and travel the way the locals used to. Great if you fancy an autumn holiday that isn't just a resort.

🥾 Walkers' favourite

Madeira

Portugal's Atlantic gem — dramatic cliffs, subtropical gardens, world-class levada walks and mild temperatures right through winter. Increasingly popular with Irish walkers and couples looking for something a bit different.

🏛️ History + sun

Malta

Late-summer weather stretches well into October in Malta — Valletta's honey-coloured streets, Blue Lagoon boat trips and some of the Mediterranean's best-preserved history. English is widely spoken, which makes it very easy for a first proper trip abroad with kids too.

☀️ October beach weather

Cyprus

October in Cyprus still delivers 27°C+ and warm sea temperatures. Paphos, Ayia Napa and the Troodos mountains — brilliant for a proper beach holiday exactly when the rest of Europe starts pulling out the woolly jumpers.

🐫 Peak Egypt season

Egypt — Cairo & Luxor

October and November are the peak season for Egypt — the summer heat finally lifts, temperatures become ideal for the pyramids, Nile cruises and Luxor's temples. One of the great trips of a lifetime and far more accessible from Ireland than most people realise.

Petra is one of the great sights of the world — a rose-red city carved into cliffs. Autumn is the perfect season to visit before winter chill sets in. Wadi Rum's desert landscapes make it even more extraordinary.

🏛️ Autumn City Breaks — Finally the Right Season

Summer crowds gone, prices settled and weather that actually lets you walk around all day. Autumn is when Europe's best city breaks stop feeling like a marathon in the heat and start feeling like proper holidays again.

🇮🇹
☀️ Autumn is perfect

Rome, Italy

Rome in October is one of Europe's great city breaks — warm days, cool evenings, thinner crowds than summer. The Vatican, the Colosseum, the Trevi Fountain and unbeatable food. A skip-the-line Vatican tour is worth every cent.

🍷 Harvest season

Porto & Douro Valley

Autumn is harvest season in the Douro Valley — the terraced vineyards look extraordinary and the port wine tastes even better. Porto itself is one of Europe's most atmospheric cities and much cheaper than Lisbon.

🌡️ Finally cool enough

Seville, Spain

Summer in Seville is brutal — but October is exactly right. Cool enough to walk the streets, wander the Alcázar and enjoy proper tapas evenings. A completely different city in autumn.

💰 Great value

Krakow, Poland

One of Europe's most affordable and moving city breaks — Krakow's Old Town, Wawel Castle and a day trip to Auschwitz for anyone who wants to properly understand European history. Autumn light on the market square is beautiful.

♨️ Thermal baths season

Budapest, Hungary

Budapest is at its best in October — the Danube looks incredible, the thermal baths steam in the cool air and there's still plenty of energy in the ruin bars. A completely different vibe to summer Budapest, and better for it.

🍂 Golden autumn

One of Europe's most beautiful cities is even better in autumn — golden light, thinner crowds and cooler temperatures for wandering. Prague Castle, Charles Bridge and the world's best value beer.

✈️ Big Trips — Autumn is the Sweet Spot

Some of the world's most incredible destinations peak in autumn — Japan's foliage rivals its cherry blossoms, and Dubai's summer heat finally breaks into perfect exploring weather.

🇯🇵
🍁 Autumn foliage (kōyō)

Japan

October and November are one of Japan's most beautiful seasons — autumn foliage (kōyō) rivals the spring cherry blossoms. Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Hiroshima. The JR rail network makes it easy to chase the colours south as they change.

🌡️ Finally cool enough

Dubai, UAE

October and November are the sweet spot for Dubai — the summer heat breaks, outdoor activities become pleasant and the winter tourist rush hasn't yet peaked. Great as a standalone break or a stopover on the way to Asia.
