Queenstown & Wanaka

Queenstown — adventure everywhere

Queenstown is often billed as New Zealand's adventure capital, and it certainly felt that way to us. Bungee jumping, skydiving, jet boating, mountain biking and hiking all sit against the backdrop of Lake Wakatipu and the surrounding mountains.

Even if you are not throwing yourself off anything, the setting alone makes Queenstown worth the stop. The lakefront, mountain views and sheer energy of the place give it a completely different feel from anywhere else on our New Zealand route.

Wanaka — a change of pace

Wanaka gave us similarly beautiful lake-and-mountain scenery with a more relaxed feel. It is an easy place to slow things down after Queenstown's energy and simply enjoy the South Island landscape.

The Crown Range Road

The road between Queenstown and Wanaka via the Crown Range is an experience in itself, with huge views across the mountains and valleys.

Worth knowing: mountain-road conditions can change quickly, so check current local road advice before setting out.
