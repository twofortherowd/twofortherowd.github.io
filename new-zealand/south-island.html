South Island —
fjords, glaciers & adventure

The South Island is where New Zealand becomes almost unreal. The dramatic fjords of Milford Sound, the adventure capital of Queenstown, the otherworldly stargazing at Lake Tekapo, and the surreal landscape of Franz Josef Glacier surrounded by rainforest. The South Island is the second half of the tour and it's extraordinary.

Kaikōura — whale watching

Kaikōura sits on the northeast coast of the South Island and is famous for whale watching. The bay is home to sperm whales and it's one of the few places in the world where you can see them relatively close to shore. The area is also spectacularly beautiful with mountains rising straight from the ocean.

Franz Josef — glacier in the rainforest

Franz Josef Glacier sits surrounded by lush rainforest in a setting that makes no sense until you're standing in it. Walking up to the glacier face with the jungle all around you is one of those genuinely surreal New Zealand moments — the kind that makes you understand why people say the country doesn't look real.

Christchurch — rebuilding and reflection

The tour finished in Christchurch — a city still rebuilding its identity after the devastating earthquakes of 2010 and 2011, but doing so with remarkable spirit and creativity. The container malls, the street art, the rebuilt cathedral precinct — Christchurch has something genuinely moving about it.
