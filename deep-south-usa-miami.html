🚗 The Road Trip Story

What a trip. What a country.

"Every stage felt different — historic East Coast cities, Colonial Williamsburg, the Blue Ridge and Smokies, Tennessee music country, New Orleans, the Gulf Coast and finally Florida."

We did the journey as an organised 13-night tour from New York to Miami, covering more than 4,000 kilometres and 14 states. The transport, hotels and overall route were taken care of, leaving us to enjoy each stop.

Some places became much bigger highlights than we expected: we loved quaint Williamsburg, Dolly Parton's Stampede was brilliant, Graceland was a standout, and New Orleans gave us both our Steamboat NATCHEZ dinner cruise and Oak Alley Plantation. Florida then brought Kennedy Space Center, the Everglades and the finish line in Miami.

💡 Practical Road Trip Info

What we'd tell a friend planning the same route

  • Watch out for one-way rental fees. If you're driving independently and picking up in New York / dropping off in Miami, one-way car rental drop-off charges can be steep — sometimes hundreds of dollars. Compare quotes carefully or consider a round-trip loop instead.
  • Fly into New York (JFK / LGA / EWR), fly out of Miami (MIA). Open-jaw flights from Dublin are perfectly doable and save you a lot of backtracking at the end of the trip.
  • Book Nashville and New Orleans hotels early. These are two of the busiest tourist towns on the route — accommodation gets scarce and expensive in peak periods.
  • The weather changes dramatically from top to bottom of the trip. Northern legs can be cool, Florida is hot and humid year-round. Pack in layers.
  • Hurricane season runs roughly June to November along the Gulf Coast and Florida. Not a reason to avoid, but worth being aware of if you're travelling in that window.
  • Nashville and New Orleans nightlife runs LATE. Don't plan an early start the next day if you're going out on Broadway or in the French Quarter.
  • Distances between stops vary a lot. Some legs are 2–3 hours, others are 6+ hours. Build in enough driving time and don't try to add more stops than the itinerary can absorb.
  • The southern leg is diner-and-BBQ territory. Embrace it — Memphis BBQ, New Orleans Cajun and Creole, Nashville hot chicken. Some of the best food we ate on the trip.

❓ Road Trip FAQ

Deep South USA FAQ

How long was our Deep South road trip?
Our route ran for 13 nights from New York to Miami and covered more than 4,000 kilometres through 14 states.
What were our biggest highlights?
Graceland was a standout, and we also loved Williamsburg, Dolly Parton's Stampede, New Orleans, Oak Alley Plantation, Kennedy Space Center and the Everglades.
Did we travel independently?
We did this route as an organised tour, which took care of the transport, accommodation and overall itinerary while still giving us time to experience each stop.
Can I do this route independently instead of on a tour?
Absolutely — many people drive this route independently. The biggest things to plan for are one-way rental car drop-off fees (which can be significant when picking up in New York and dropping off in Miami), booking accommodation well ahead in busy stops like Nashville and New Orleans, and building in enough driving time between stops. If you'd rather have the transport and hotels sorted for you, we did ours as an organised tour.
What is the best time of year to drive New York to Miami?
Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November, with a caveat about hurricane season) are generally the best windows — mild temperatures right along the route, and the Deep South and Florida haven't hit peak humidity yet. Summer is doable but very hot in the South. Winter works well for Florida but the northern legs can be cold, and mountain routes through the Smokies can be affected by weather.
How many nights should we allow for this route?
We did it in 13 nights and that felt right — enough time to properly explore the big stops (New York, Washington DC, Nashville, Memphis, New Orleans, Miami) while still keeping momentum. You could compress it into 10 or 11 nights if you cut a stop or two, or extend to 16–18 to add extra nights in New Orleans and Miami. Under 10 nights and you're spending too much time driving.
Where can I read the individual city guides?
This page is the road-trip hub. Our separate guides currently cover New York, Philadelphia, Washington DC, Williamsburg, Gatlinburg and the Great Smoky Mountains, Nashville, Memphis, New Orleans, Kennedy Space Center, the Everglades and Miami.
