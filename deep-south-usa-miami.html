🚗 The Road Trip Story

What a trip. What a country.

"Every stage felt different — historic East Coast cities, Colonial Williamsburg, the Blue Ridge and Smokies, Tennessee music country, New Orleans, the Gulf Coast and finally Florida."

We did the journey as an organised 13-night tour from New York to Miami, covering more than 4,000 kilometres and 14 states. The transport, hotels and overall route were taken care of, leaving us to enjoy each stop.

Some places became much bigger highlights than we expected: we loved quaint Williamsburg, Dolly Parton's Stampede was brilliant, Graceland was a standout, and New Orleans gave us both our Steamboat NATCHEZ dinner cruise and Oak Alley Plantation. Florida then brought Kennedy Space Center, the Everglades and the finish line in Miami.

📅 The Route

13 nights — New York to Miami

Opening leg
🗽 New York → Philadelphia → Washington DC

We started in New York, stopped in Philadelphia and continued to Washington DC.

Virginia
🏘️ Washington DC → Williamsburg → Roanoke

Williamsburg was one of the loveliest surprises of the trip before the route continued west through Virginia and Roanoke.

Mountains & Tennessee
🏔️ Roanoke → Gatlinburg & Great Smoky Mountains → Nashville → Memphis

The Blue Ridge and Smokies led us into Tennessee, Dolly Parton's Stampede, Nashville's country music and then Memphis and Graceland.

Deep South
🎶 Memphis → Jackson → New Orleans

We travelled through Jackson before reaching New Orleans, where our stay included the Steamboat NATCHEZ dinner cruise and Oak Alley Plantation.

Gulf Coast & Florida
🏖️ New Orleans → Pensacola → Orlando → Kennedy Space Center → Miami

The final leg crossed the Gulf Coast into Florida, with Orlando and Kennedy Space Center before the journey finished in Miami. Our Everglades visit belongs to the South Florida part of the trip rather than being a separate overnight stop on the route.
