If you think the midlands are just somewhere you drive through on the way to somewhere else, Offaly might change your mind!

From one of Ireland's most important monastic sites to a castle with a remarkable place in the history of astronomy, Offaly has plenty of fascinating stories. Add bogland sculpture trails, the Slieve Bloom Mountains, Shannon towns and a few ghostly legends, and you've got a fantastic adventure.

And the best bit? You can explore loads without battling crowds of tourists.

Clonmacnoise, Birr & the Shannon
Monastic site

✝️ Clonmacnoise

Founded by St Ciarán in the sixth century on the banks of the Shannon, Clonmacnoise became one of the great centres of learning in Europe. Explore churches, round towers and magnificent high crosses, with the originals protected in the visitor centre.

Why visit? Archaeology, Irish history and culture.

Heritage Ireland ↗
Gardens · Science

🔭 Birr Castle Demesne

Gardens, lakes and woodland around a castle still lived in by the Parsons family. The highlight is the Great Telescope, the 'Leviathan of Parsonstown', the largest telescope in the world when it was built in 1845.

Why visit? Gardens, families and science.

Heritage town

🏘️ Birr

Elegant Georgian streets, malls and squares, and a relaxed town-centre atmosphere.

Why visit? Architecture, cafés and relaxed exploring.

Shannon town

⛵ Banagher

A traditional Shannon town with boating heritage. Anthony Trollope lived here, and Charlotte Brontë spent part of her honeymoon here.

Why visit? River trips and local history.

Shannon village

🌉 Shannonbridge

A village beside the Shannon, close to Clonmacnoise.

Why visit? River scenery.

Around the county
Bogs, mountains & castles
Bog park · Sculpture

🌾 Lough Boora Discovery Park

A former peat-harvesting landscape transformed into a nature park, with sculptures, walking and cycling trails and important wildlife habitats.

Why visit? Families, nature and art.

Mountains

⛰️ The Slieve Bloom Mountains

Shared with Laois, these peaceful mountains have forest walks, waterfalls and upland views.

Why visit? Walking, cycling and nature.

Raised bog · Nature reserve

🌿 Clara Bog

One of the best remaining raised bogs in Western Europe, with a boardwalk and visitor centre.

Why visit? Nature and environmental heritage.

⚠️ Stay on the boardwalk and designated paths.
Gothic castle · Tours

🏰 Charleville Castle

A spectacular Gothic Revival castle in ancient woodland near Tullamore, with volunteer-led tours (including ghost tours) at various times.

Why visit? Architecture and folklore.

📅 Check tour times with the castle before you go.
Distillery

🥃 Tullamore D.E.W.

Tours of the Tullamore D.E.W. distillery outside the town tell the story of the whiskey. The old heritage centre on Bury Quay closed in 2020.

Why visit? Adults interested in whiskey.

🎟️ Check current tour and booking arrangements.
Haunted castle · Private

👻 Leap Castle

Often called the most haunted castle in Ireland. It's a private family home.

Why visit? Folklore and unusual heritage.

🚫 Visits only by arrangement with the owners. Don't just turn up.

History, myth & mystery
Myths, legends & history

History and legend, clearly labelled.

Legend

👻 The ghosts of Leap Castle

Legend says one O'Carroll brother killed another, a priest, at the altar of the chapel, which became known as the Bloody Chapel. Later a hidden dungeon full of bones was reportedly found, and the castle is said to be haunted by a foul-smelling presence known as 'the Elemental'.

History

✝️ Clonmacnoise

Kings were buried here, scholars came from across Europe, and the monastery survived Viking raids and Norman attacks before falling into ruin. Pope John Paul II visited in 1979.

Science

🔭 The Leviathan of Parsonstown

William Parsons, the 3rd Earl of Rosse, built the Great Telescope in 1845, and it remained the largest in the world until 1917. Through it, he was the first to see the spiral structure of what we now know are galaxies.

Archaeology

🌾 Bog bodies

Irish bogs preserve the past incredibly well. Old Croghan Man, found near Croghan Hill in 2003, is now on display at the National Museum in Dublin.

Common questions
County Offaly FAQ
Can you visit Leap Castle?
Only by arrangement with the owners, as it's a private home. Don't just turn up.
Can you still see the Great Telescope at Birr?
Yes, it's been restored and stands in the grounds of Birr Castle Demesne.
Is the Tullamore D.E.W. heritage centre open?
The old Bury Quay heritage centre closed in 2020. Tours now run at the distillery, so check current arrangements.
