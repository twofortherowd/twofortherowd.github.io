Our Ireland road-trip tips
  • Leave room for detours. Some of our favourite discoveries began with a sign, a gate or a road we'd never taken before.
  • Download maps before rural drives. Mobile coverage can still disappear exactly when you decide to leave the main road.
  • Watch for heritage signs. Ireland is packed with ruins, monastic sites, gardens, castles and monuments that barely advertise themselves.
  • Bring waterproof layers. Sunshine at breakfast tells you very little about what the afternoon is going to do.
  • Don't judge distance by kilometres alone. Smaller roads, tractors, villages and photo stops can make a short-looking journey take much longer.
  • Ask people locally. A recommendation from somebody behind a bar, café counter or shop till can be better than an hour of scrolling.

Common questions
Ireland travel FAQ
What are the best places to visit in Ireland?
There isn't one perfect list. Ireland's famous places are famous for a reason, but we think some of the best trips mix them with smaller towns, heritage sites and unexpected stops. That's why this hub is organised around the Ireland we've actually explored rather than a generic top-ten list.
What parts of Ireland do tourists miss?
The Midlands and South Leinster are regularly overlooked beside Dublin and the west coast. Our Hidden Ireland collection is specifically about the places that deserve more attention.
Do I need a car to explore Ireland?
For major cities, no. For the kind of Ireland we love exploring — rural heritage sites, gardens, small towns and spontaneous stops — a car makes a huge difference.
What is the best time of year to visit Ireland?
Late spring through early autumn gives longer daylight for road trips, while spring and autumn can be quieter. The weather is changeable year-round, so we plan around what we want to see rather than waiting for a guaranteed sunny week!
Where can I find unusual places in Ireland?
Start with our Hidden Ireland guide. It's where we collect the castles, ruins, gardens, quirky attractions and overlooked places that have surprised us most.
