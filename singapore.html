The story

Nicola & Anita — Singapore in 2012

Two nights on the way to Australia. That was the plan. And two nights is not a lot of time in a city as dense as Singapore, so Nicola and Anita hit the ground running — a private guide for the day so they could actually see the place properly, and then everything else crammed in around it.

The food was the first revelation. Singapore's hawker centres deserve every bit of their reputation — some of the best food anywhere in the world, at prices that made us double-check the bill. Laksa, chicken rice, roti prata, satay — whatever we could get our hands on.

The Singapore Zoo Night Safari was the standout experience of the whole trip. Watching nocturnal animals in their natural setting after dark, with a properly done light show, is genuinely unlike anything else. If you have even one evening in Singapore, put it at the top of the list — and book in advance, it fills up.

Then karaoke. Anita has a lovely voice and got up to sing. Nicola sensibly concentrated on the cocktails. 🍸 That's what a Singapore evening looks like.

What we'd do differently: stay longer. Two nights was a brilliant taste, but Singapore has more than enough going on to easily fill a week.

Where we stayed

🏨 The Fullerton Bay Hotel

We stayed at The Fullerton Bay Hotel Singapore and absolutely loved it. Waterfront location, incredible Marina Bay views, a rooftop infinity pool that ranks among the best pools we've ever swum in, and Lantern — the rooftop bar — which is a gorgeous place for a drink overlooking the skyline as the city lights come on.

Would we stay there again? Absolutely, without hesitation.

Common questions

Singapore FAQ

Is Singapore worth visiting on a stopover?
Yes. Two nights is enough to get a proper taste of the city — the food scene alone is worth it. Add a private city tour and the Singapore Zoo Night Safari and you have an excellent introduction to one of the most interesting cities in the world. We'd go back for a full week without hesitation.
Is the Singapore Zoo Night Safari worth it?
Yes — this was the standout experience of our whole two-night stopover. Watching nocturnal animals in their natural setting after dark, with a properly done light show, is genuinely unlike anything else. Book in advance as it fills up fast.
How long do you need in Singapore?
Two nights gives you a brilliant taste — enough for a private city tour, the night safari, a rooftop drink and plenty of hawker food. Three to five days lets you explore properly. A week would let you really slow down. Honestly, we'd go back for a full week without hesitation.
What is the local food like in Singapore?
Extraordinary. Singapore has one of the best food scenes anywhere in the world. The hawker centres are legendary — incredibly good food at very reasonable prices. Laksa, chicken rice, chilli crab, roti prata and satay are all must-tries.
Is Singapore easy for gluten-free travellers?
Rice-based dishes make gluten-free eating in Singapore fairly straightforward — laksa, chicken rice, satay and many hawker options are naturally gluten-free. The one thing to check carefully is soy sauce, which typically contains wheat unless it's specifically a gluten-free version. Ask before you order.
