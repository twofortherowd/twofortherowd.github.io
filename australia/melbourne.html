🎡 Our Melbourne Experience

A revelation of a city

Melbourne was a revelation. Lots of Irish people, a brilliant food and café scene, beautiful architecture and a character completely different to Sydney.

We noticed a strong Irish presence in Melbourne, which gave parts of the city a familiar feel. Add the cafés, food scene, laneways, street art and live music and there is plenty to explore beyond the headline sights.

🐧 Phillip Island Little Penguins

The little penguins at dusk

A highlight was Phillip Island's little penguins — watching the tiny penguins waddle up the beach at dusk was one of those genuinely magical natural experiences that stays with you for years.

Phillip Island is about 90 minutes south-east of Melbourne and the Penguin Parade at Summerland Beach is one of Australia's most beloved wildlife experiences. The penguins are wild — the parade happens every evening at sunset, entirely on the penguins' own schedule.

🚁 Twelve Apostles Helicopter Flight

One of the great upgrades of the trip

One of the great moments of our entire Australia trip was seeing the Twelve Apostles by helicopter on the Great Ocean Road. We did the helicopter flight ourselves and absolutely loved it — seeing those enormous limestone stacks rising from the Southern Ocean from the air was extraordinary. It gave us a completely different perspective on one of Australia's most iconic stretches of coastline and was easily one of the standout experiences of the trip.

🛣️ The Great Ocean Road

One of the world's great drives

The Great Ocean Road stretches roughly 240km along Victoria's south-west coast and is one of the most celebrated coastal drives on the planet. Every bend gives another view — dramatic cliffs, hidden coves, surf beaches, eucalyptus rainforest and koalas in the trees at Kennett River.

The Twelve Apostles are the road's most famous stop, but the whole drive from Torquay through Lorne, Apollo Bay and beyond is the real experience. Renting a car and taking your time is the best way to do it.

💡 Practical Tips

Melbourne practical info

  • Getting there: Melbourne Tullamarine Airport connects to almost everywhere. SkyBus runs from the airport to the city.
  • Getting around: Melbourne has one of the world's best tram networks. The myki card covers trams, trains and buses.
  • Phillip Island: About 90 minutes' drive south-east. Book Penguin Parade tickets in advance — visitor numbers are managed.
  • Great Ocean Road: Two days is much better than a rushed one — an overnight in Lorne or Apollo Bay lets you actually enjoy the drive.
  • Best time to visit: March-May (autumn) and September-November (spring). Melbourne's weather is famously changeable — four seasons in a day is a real phrase here.

Common questions

FAQ

Is Melbourne worth visiting?
Absolutely — Melbourne is one of Australia's most liveable and culturally rich cities. Great food, brilliant coffee, laneways full of hidden bars and a large warm Irish community. A very different vibe to Sydney and well worth several nights.
Is the Twelve Apostles helicopter flight worth it?
Yes — seeing the Twelve Apostles from the air is one of those upgrades that is absolutely worth every cent. The rock stacks are spectacular from the cliff tops but from a helicopter they are extraordinary.
Are the Phillip Island little penguins worth going to see?
Yes — hundreds of tiny penguins waddling up the beach at dusk is one of Australia's most genuinely magical wildlife experiences. Book in advance as visitor numbers are managed.
How long should you spend in Melbourne?
Three to five nights lets you cover the city plus a Phillip Island day and a Great Ocean Road trip. A full week is very easy to fill with day trips into the Yarra Valley and beyond.
Can you do the Great Ocean Road in a day from Melbourne?
You can, but two days is much better. An overnight in Lorne or Apollo Bay lets you actually enjoy the drive, the beaches and the rainforest sections rather than rushing to the Twelve Apostles.
