🏛️ Our Canberra Experience

A stop on the road south

After Sydney, we rented a car and drove south — stopping in Canberra for a day on the way to Melbourne. Australia's purpose-built capital is genuinely interesting — the parliament buildings, the National War Memorial, the beautiful lake at the heart of the planned city. Worth a day if you're driving between Sydney and Melbourne.

"Canberra is one of those places you might not put on your Australia bucket list at first — but if you are driving between Sydney and Melbourne, a day here is a really nice change of pace from beach towns and big-city buzz. It has a completely different character."

Canberra was designed from scratch in the early 20th century as a compromise capital between Sydney and Melbourne, and the whole city is laid out with axes, geometry and long sight lines. That planned quality gives it a very distinctive feel — spacious, green and calm.

⭐ Things to Do in Canberra

Canberra highlights

🏛️
Parliament House

The seat of Australian government — free public tours run daily and the roof lawn is one of the great city viewpoints.

🎖️
Australian War Memorial

One of the most moving national war memorials anywhere in the world. Free entry, extraordinary exhibits.

💧
Lake Burley Griffin

The huge artificial lake at the centre of the city — walk, cycle or take a ferry across it.

🖼️
National Gallery of Australia

Home to the country's most significant art collection including major Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander works.

🏔️
Mount Ainslie lookout

The best view over Canberra — you can see the whole planned axis of the city laid out below you.

🇦🇺
National Museum of Australia

Free-entry museum on the story of Australia and its people — genuinely well done.

💡 Practical Tips

Canberra practical info

  • Getting there: Canberra is roughly a 3-hour drive south of Sydney or a 7-hour drive north of Melbourne. Canberra Airport connects to major Australian cities.
  • Getting around: A car makes life much easier — the city is very spread out. Rideshare works fine for shorter hops.
  • How long to stay: One full day covers the essentials. Two days lets you see the major museums and galleries at a proper pace.
  • Best time to visit: Autumn (March-May) is spectacular for the leaves. Winter can be surprisingly cold — Canberra is inland and gets frost.
🚗 On the Road South

Sydney → Canberra → Melbourne

Canberra makes a worthwhile detour on a Sydney-to-Melbourne road trip. From Sydney, it is roughly a three-hour drive south-west; from Canberra you can continue towards Melbourne, rejoining the main Hume Highway route further south.

Common questions

FAQ

Is Canberra worth visiting?
Yes — as a stop on the drive between Sydney and Melbourne, Canberra earns a day. The parliament buildings, the Australian War Memorial and the lake at the centre of the planned city are all genuinely interesting.
How long do you need in Canberra?
One full day covers the essentials — Parliament House, the Australian War Memorial and a walk around Lake Burley Griffin. Two days lets you dig into the museums and galleries properly.
How do you get to Canberra?
About a 3-hour drive south of Sydney or 7 hours north of Melbourne. Canberra Airport also connects to major Australian cities. A car is by far the easiest way to get around once you're there.
What is Canberra known for?
Being Australia's purpose-built capital — chosen as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne. Parliament House, the Australian War Memorial and the National Gallery are the headline attractions.
