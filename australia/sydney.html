🌉 Our Sydney Story

Sunshine, the Opera House and two trips to Palm Beach

Sydney is everything it's supposed to be — the Opera House is as magnificent in person as in every photograph, the harbour is stunning and the sunshine is relentless. The buzz of arriving in Sydney for the first time is something genuinely special.

"Sydney is one of those cities where the postcards are actually accurate. You walk around the corner, see the Opera House and the Harbour Bridge for the first time, and it hits you exactly the way it's supposed to."

Beyond the Home and Away pilgrimage and the Opera House moment, Sydney gave brilliant beach time at Bondi, the harbour walk, great food and that easy Australian warmth that makes the city feel immediately welcoming to Irish visitors.

🎭 Opera at the Sydney Opera House

Bucket list, ticked

Going to see an opera at the Sydney Opera House was a bucket list moment done properly — inside the building is as extraordinary as outside it, and the experience of being in one of the world's most iconic buildings for a performance is something that stays with you.

The Opera House is not just one venue — it holds several performance halls, and even if opera is not your thing there is usually a concert, ballet, symphony or theatre production running that lets you experience the interior. We strongly recommend doing more than a photo out front.

📺 Palm Beach — Twice, for Home and Away

The Home and Away pilgrimage

📺 The Palm Beach situation: Went to Palm Beach day one — missed the Home and Away cast. Went back day two. Saw the cast. Saw Brax specifically. Was absolutely delighted. Zero regrets about going twice. This is what travel is about. 😄

Palm Beach is a beautiful spot in its own right — a long crescent of golden sand at the very northern tip of Sydney's Northern Beaches, backed by the pines and headland that anyone who has ever watched Home and Away will recognise instantly.

For Irish visitors who grew up watching the show, standing on Summer Bay in real life is a proper pilgrimage moment. Our determination to actually see the cast is exactly the level of commitment the trip deserved — and going back a second day to catch Brax was absolutely worth it. We can confirm.

🏖️ Bondi & Sydney's Beaches

Bondi, the harbour and the Sydney way of life

Bondi Beach is the beach every visitor to Sydney has to see — the crescent of sand, the iconic Bondi Icebergs pool at the southern end and the walking track that leads all the way to Coogee. It's busy, it's loud, it's iconic and it's exactly what a Sydney beach should be.

Beyond Bondi, Sydney is genuinely a city of beaches — Manly across the harbour, the Northern Beaches curving up to Palm Beach, the coves and inlets of Sydney Harbour itself. It's part of why Sydney feels so different from most other big cities.

🏔️ The Blue Mountains

Out of this world

The Blue Mountains were breathtaking — absolutely out of this world. Driving through them with the vast green valleys dropping away on all sides and the haze hanging in the eucalyptus air is one of those experiences that stays with you.

The mountains are an easy day trip from Sydney (roughly 90 minutes' drive west) and give a completely different side of New South Wales — sandstone cliffs, deep gorges, the famous Three Sisters rock formation and endless views out over the eucalyptus forests that give the range its distinctive blue haze.

💡 Practical Tips

Sydney practical info

  • Getting there: Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport is well connected internationally and one train ride from the city centre.
  • Getting around: The Opal card covers ferries, trains, buses and light rail. Ferries are as much sightseeing as transport.
  • Palm Beach: Roughly 1 hour drive from central Sydney. Palm Beach ferry and bus options exist but a car makes the day much easier.
  • Blue Mountains: Katoomba is about 90 minutes by car or train. Many good day-trip tours from central Sydney include the Three Sisters and Scenic World.
  • Best time to visit: Spring (September to November) and autumn (March to May) — mild, sunny and less crowded than summer.

Common questions

FAQ

Is the Sydney Opera House worth going inside?
Absolutely — the inside of the Opera House is every bit as extraordinary as the outside. See a performance if you can, whether opera, ballet, orchestra or theatre. We saw an opera there and rate it as a proper bucket list moment.
Is Palm Beach worth visiting for Home and Away fans?
Absolutely — and go twice if you miss the cast on the first visit, like we did! Palm Beach is a beautiful spot in its own right but for anyone who grew up watching Home and Away it is a proper pilgrimage moment. Brax was there on the second visit.
How long should you spend in Sydney?
Three to five nights is a comfortable minimum — enough for the harbour, Bondi, the Opera House, a Blue Mountains day trip and Palm Beach. A week lets you slow down and see more of the beaches and neighbourhoods.
Are the Blue Mountains worth a day trip from Sydney?
Yes — the Blue Mountains are breathtaking. About 90 minutes west of the city, they give you a completely different side of New South Wales with sandstone cliffs, deep valleys and the famous Three Sisters rock formation.
How do you get to Palm Beach from Sydney?
It's about an hour's drive from central Sydney up the Northern Beaches. There are also public buses and a ferry from Ettalong on the Central Coast, but a car makes the day much easier — especially if you might want to come back a second time.
