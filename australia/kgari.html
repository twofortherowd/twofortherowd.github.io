🏝️ Our K'gari Experience

The world's largest sand island

K'gari — formerly known as Fraser Island — was gorgeous. The world's largest sand island, with freshwater lakes sitting incongruously in the middle of sand dunes, dingoes roaming the beaches and a landscape that looks like it shouldn't exist but absolutely does. A brilliant stop that often gets overlooked by people racing between Cairns and Sydney.

The island is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and completely unique — 120km long, made entirely of sand, and yet home to towering rainforest, freshwater dune lakes, wild dingoes and some of the most beautiful beaches in Queensland.

📛 A Note on the Name

K'gari — the proper name restored

The island was known as Fraser Island for many years but has officially been restored to its original Butchulla name — K'gari (pronounced roughly "gurri"), which means "paradise". The Butchulla are the traditional custodians of the island and the name change reflects a broader recognition of Aboriginal cultural heritage across Australia.

Some tour operators, guidebooks and older maps may still use "Fraser Island" — they refer to the same place.

⭐ Things to Do on K'gari

K'gari highlights

💧
Lake McKenzie

The most famous of K'gari's perched dune lakes — impossibly clear, ringed by silica sand. Iconic.

🏖️
75 Mile Beach

The beach that doubles as the island's highway — 4WD only, and one of the most extraordinary stretches of sand anywhere.

🚢
Maheno Shipwreck

The rusting hull of a WWI hospital ship stranded on 75 Mile Beach — one of the island's most photographed sights.

🌊
Champagne Pools

Natural rock pools where ocean waves wash over the rocks and create their famous bubbling effect; always follow local safety advice and conditions.

🌲
Central Station rainforest

Ancient rainforest growing straight out of pure sand — genuinely otherworldly.

🐕
Dingoes

K'gari is home to some of the purest strains of dingoes in Australia — spotted from a safe distance across the beaches.

💡 Practical Tips

K'gari practical info

  • Getting there: Barge access from Hervey Bay or Rainbow Beach on the Queensland mainland. Most East Coast tours include the ferry.
  • Only 4WD vehicles can drive on the island — the "roads" are all sand. Most visitors go on a guided 4WD tour rather than self-driving.
  • Dingo safety: never feed, approach or turn your back on a dingo. They are wild animals and warnings are taken very seriously by park rangers.
  • Ocean swimming: conditions around K'gari can be hazardous, with strong currents and other risks. Follow current local and ranger advice and use designated swimming areas where applicable.
  • When to visit: conditions vary through the year, so choose your timing based on the weather and activities you want to do.

Common questions

FAQ

Is K'gari worth visiting?
Yes — K'gari is one of those genuinely unique places. The world's largest sand island, freshwater lakes sitting in the middle of sand dunes, ancient rainforest and wild dingoes. It is gorgeous and often overlooked by people racing up and down the East Coast.
What is the difference between K'gari and Fraser Island?
They are the same place. Fraser Island has officially been renamed K'gari, using the Butchulla people's traditional name for the island (meaning 'paradise'). Older signs, guidebooks and tours may still use Fraser Island.
Can you swim at K'gari?
Freshwater lakes and the Champagne Pools are popular swimming spots, subject to current conditions and local guidance. Ocean conditions around K'gari can be hazardous, so always follow ranger and local safety advice.
How do you get around K'gari?
Only 4WD vehicles can access the island because all the roads are sand. Most visitors go on a guided 4WD tour from the mainland rather than self-driving.
