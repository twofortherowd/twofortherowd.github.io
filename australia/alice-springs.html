🤠 Our Alice Springs Experience

A frontier town in the middle of the Outback

After New Zealand, we travelled into Alice Springs for our Northern Territory adventure — and this part of the trip turned out to be just as extraordinary as anything that had come before it.

Alice Springs is completely unlike the rest of Australia. A frontier town in the middle of the Outback, surrounded by the MacDonnell Ranges, with a distinct character all of its own.

🚑 Royal Flying Doctor Service

One of the most fascinating stops of the trip

The Royal Flying Doctor Service museum was genuinely fascinating — the story of how medical care reaches the most remote communities in one of the world's most sparsely populated landscapes is extraordinary.

The Flying Doctors were founded in the 1920s to bring aeromedical care to Outback stations, cattle properties and Aboriginal communities separated by hundreds of kilometres of harsh terrain from the nearest hospital. Today they still operate one of the largest aeromedical fleets in the world.

💡 Practical Tips

Alice Springs practical info

  • Getting there: Alice Springs Airport has direct flights from most major Australian cities. Flying is by far the easiest way in — driving from the coast takes days.
  • To Uluru: Uluru is roughly 5.5 hours by road from Alice Springs. Many Northern Territory tours combine both.
  • Weather: Central Australia has huge temperature swings. Days can be very hot even in winter; nights are surprisingly cold. Layers matter.
  • Best time to visit: April to September — the dry, cooler months. December to February is punishingly hot.
  • Respect Aboriginal cultural sites throughout the region — many are sacred and photography or access rules should be observed.

Common questions

FAQ

Is Alice Springs worth visiting?
Yes — Alice Springs is completely unlike the rest of Australia. Out in the Red Centre, surrounded by the vast Outback, with a distinct frontier town character all of its own. The Royal Flying Doctor Service museum is genuinely fascinating, the saloon bars are brilliant fun and the landscape around the town is extraordinary.
How do you get to Alice Springs?
Fly. Alice Springs Airport connects to most major Australian cities. Driving from the coast takes days across some of the most remote roads in the country.
How far is Uluru from Alice Springs?
Roughly 5.5 hours by road, or a short domestic flight. Many Northern Territory tours combine Alice Springs and Uluru into one trip.
When is the best time to visit Alice Springs?
April to September — the dry, cooler months. Days are pleasant to warm and nights are cold. December to February is punishingly hot and best avoided.
