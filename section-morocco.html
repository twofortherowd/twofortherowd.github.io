Quick facts before you go 🇲🇦

Nothing encyclopaedic here — just a few useful things worth knowing before you book.

🏙️ Capital
Rabat — although Marrakech is one of Morocco's major tourism hubs and where our trip began.
🗣️ Language
Arabic and Amazigh are the official languages. Moroccan Arabic (Darija) is widely spoken, and you'll hear plenty of French too.
💵 Currency
Moroccan Dirham (MAD). Bring cash. Seriously. It's particularly handy in souks, smaller shops and cafés.
🕐 Time
Morocco is usually close to Irish/UK time, but its clocks can change around Ramadan — check before you fly.
🔌 Plugs
European-style two-pin plugs are commonly used, so coming from Ireland you'll want an adaptor.
🌡️ When to go
Spring and autumn are good choices if you'd rather avoid the most intense summer heat.
