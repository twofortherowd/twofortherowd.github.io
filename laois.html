Why this county matters to us
Our Laois

Laois has been part of Nicola's story from the very start. She grew up near Portlaoise, and some of her clearest childhood memories are the simplest ones: Sunday afternoons meant a walk in the Slieve Bloom Mountains, and autumn often meant heading out to the Heath to pick hazelnuts. Family trips up to the Rock of Dunamase were always a highlight too — the kind of place you never quite get tired of, however many times you've been.

Years later, exploring Laois with Podge has added a different layer to that. We've walked the woods around Stradbally together more than once, filmed the round tower and Heritage Festival in Timahoe, and taken a guided tour through Stradbally Hall — home to the Cosby family since the 1600s and better known to most people as the site of Electric Picnic.

For us, Laois isn't a county you visit for one big landmark. It's Sunday walks, family days out, heritage doors that don't normally open, and woods you find yourself going back to without really planning it.

This is the county Nicola grew up in. We're still finding new corners of it together.

The Laois Nicola grew up with
Childhood favourites near Portlaoise
The family day out

🏰 Rock of Dunamase

A rocky outcrop just outside Portlaoise topped with the ruins of a Hiberno-Norman hilltop castle. Family trips here were always a highlight of Nicola's childhood, and it's easy to see why — the views out over the Laois countryside towards the Slieve Blooms are hard to beat.

Sunday afternoons

⛰️ Slieve Bloom Mountains

Sunday meant a walk in the Slieve Blooms more often than not. The mountains straddle the Laois-Offaly border, with waterfalls, forest trails and blanket bog making up some of the county's best walking country.

Autumn tradition

🌰 The Heath

A stretch of common and woodland just outside Portlaoise, and the spot for autumn hazelnut picking growing up. It's not a polished tourist attraction — it's the kind of place that means something because you grew up going there.

Home base

🏘️ Portlaoise

The county town, and the place Nicola calls home. Not always the first name people think of in Laois, but everything else in this guide is a short drive from here.

04
Where we keep going back
The woods around Stradbally

Some of our favourite Laois walks together aren't famous landmarks at all — they're the woods just outside Stradbally that we've walked more than once. Oughaval Woods, on the Stradbally-to-Carlow road, has a mix of looped trails through mainly broadleaf woodland, including a longer loop that passes the ruin of Cobbler's Castle, a folly built during the Famine. Nearby, Carrigmeal Woodlands is a newer addition — a young community woodland with a timber viewing tower looking out over the Slieve Blooms and across to the Rock of Dunamase.

Neither is the kind of place that needs a special occasion. They're just the woods we go back to.

Timahoe, Stradbally Hall & hidden heritage
One of Ireland's finest

🗼 Timahoe Round Tower

A 12th-century round tower standing almost 30 metres tall, with one of the most elaborately carved Romanesque doorways of any round tower in the country. We've filmed here ourselves — it's a genuinely special spot for somewhere so quiet.

Heritage Week discovery

🎉 Timahoe Heritage Festival

We caught the local Heritage Festival in Timahoe too — exactly the kind of community event that brings a small village and its history to life for a weekend.

Home of the Cosby family

🏛️ Stradbally Hall

A Georgian mansion home to the Cosby family since the 1620s, set in 550 acres of woodland and parkland. It's best known nationally as the site of Electric Picnic and the National Steam Rally, but during Heritage Week it also opens for family-led guided tours of the house itself.

One thing we don't want to do on Two for the Row'd is pretend we've personally experienced somewhere when we haven't. So this section is deliberately our Laois wish list.

Next-time candidate

🌿 Abbeyleix Bog

A 500-acre community-restored raised bog on the edge of Abbeyleix, with boardwalk trails through a genuinely rare habitat. It's firmly on our list — when we visit, it moves into the first-hand part of this guide.

Next-time candidate

🏚️ Donaghmore Famine Workhouse Museum

A restored 1840s workhouse near Portlaoise telling the story of the families who lived and died there during the Great Famine. We haven't been yet, but it's on the list.

What we'd tell a friend
Our practical Laois tips
🚗 Give yourself a car if you can.
Laois's best mix of heritage stops, woods and viewpoints is much easier when you can detour whenever something catches your eye.
🏰 Don't skip the Rock of Dunamase.
It's not the biggest castle ruin in Ireland, but the setting and the views make it one of Laois's genuine highlights.
🗼 Check opening times for heritage sites.
Places like Timahoe's heritage centre and Stradbally Hall's house tours often run seasonally or during Heritage Week specifically.
⛰️ Wrap up for the Slieve Blooms.
Mountain weather changes fast, even on what looks like a mild midlands day.
🧭 Watch for Heritage Week.
Events can open doors that normally stay closed — our Stradbally Hall visit is a good example.
💎 Mix famous and personal.
Our ideal Laois day is one well-known stop, one woodland walk and at least one place that means something to us personally.

Common questions
County Laois FAQ
What is the Rock of Dunamase?
The Rock of Dunamase is a rocky outcrop near Portlaoise, County Laois, topped with the ruins of a hilltop castle dating from the early Hiberno-Norman period. It's one of the county's best-known landmarks and was a regular family day out for Nicola growing up.
Is Timahoe Round Tower worth visiting?
We think so. Timahoe's 12th-century round tower has one of the most elaborately carved Romanesque doorways of any round tower in Ireland, and we've filmed there ourselves, including during the local Heritage Festival.
Can you visit Stradbally Hall?
Stradbally Hall, home to the Cosby family, offers guided tours led by a family member during National Heritage Week. Outside of that, it is best known as the site of Electric Picnic and the National Steam Rally.
What towns are near Nicola's home area of Laois?
Nicola grew up near Portlaoise, the county town of Laois. Stradbally, Timahoe and the Rock of Dunamase are all within a short drive of Portlaoise.
Have you visited Abbeyleix Bog or Donaghmore Famine Workhouse Museum?
Not yet! Both are on our list, but we haven't personally visited, so we're keeping them in our future-Laois section until we do.
Do you have a Laois guide I can use?
Yes — we've put together our own printable Laois local guide covering attractions, places to eat, coffee stops, pubs, shopping, hidden gems and easy day trips. You'll find it further up this page.
