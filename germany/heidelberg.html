Our visit
Heidelberg on the Neckar

Castle, Old Town and the Neckar

Heidelberg Castle sits in ruins above the town, looking out over the river Neckar and the surrounding valley — the kind of view that explains why the city has such a reputation for romance. Below it, the Old Town's cobbled streets and the centuries-old university give the whole place a lived-in, storybook atmosphere that few German cities match.

It's a smaller city than Cologne, Berlin or Munich, and doesn't need a long stay to leave an impression — but it's absolutely worth building time into any Germany trip for.

Common questions
Heidelberg FAQ
Is Heidelberg worth visiting?
Yes — Heidelberg is like something out of a fairytale. The ruined castle above the old town, the river Neckar winding through the valley, the cobbled streets and the university atmosphere make it one of the most romantic and visually stunning cities in Germany.
How many days do you need in Heidelberg?
Heidelberg works beautifully as a short stay. One full day lets you see the main historic centre and castle area, while two days gives you more time to slow down and enjoy the river and Old Town.
Is Heidelberg a walkable city?
The historic centre is compact enough to explore largely on foot, which is one of the reasons Heidelberg works so well for a shorter city break.
What is Heidelberg best known for?
Heidelberg is especially known for its ruined hilltop castle, romantic Old Town, River Neckar setting and historic university atmosphere.
Is Heidelberg good for a day trip?
Yes. Its relatively compact historic centre means Heidelberg can work very well as a day trip, although staying overnight gives you more time to enjoy the city at a slower pace.
