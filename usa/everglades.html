❤️ Our Everglades Experience

Wild Florida

By the time we reached the Everglades, we'd experienced everything from historic East Coast cities and Tennessee music to New Orleans and Kennedy Space Center. South Florida changed the scenery yet again.

The Everglades are all about the landscape and wildlife. This vast wetland ecosystem is home to both American alligators and American crocodiles, as well as hundreds of bird species and an extraordinary variety of other wildlife.

For us, this was Florida at its wildest — and a brilliant contrast to the urban stops elsewhere on the trip.

🌿 What Makes the Everglades Special

A landscape unlike anywhere else on our route

🐊
Alligators & Crocodiles

The Everglades are one of the rare places where American alligators and American crocodiles share the same wider ecosystem.

🦅
Wildlife

The ecosystem supports more than 350 species of birds along with mammals, reptiles and fish.

🌾
River of Grass

The Everglades are a vast, low-lying wetland rather than the kind of dramatic mountain landscape people often associate with national parks.

🚤
Airboat Country

Airboats are strongly associated with the wider Everglades experience, although their use inside the national park itself is restricted to designated areas.

Wildlife safety Alligators and crocodiles are wild animals. National Park Service guidance is to keep a safe distance and never feed, touch or harass wildlife.

❓ Good to Know

Everglades FAQ

Where are the Everglades in relation to Miami?
The Everglades stretch across South Florida, and Miami is a popular base for visiting the eastern side of the region.
Can you take an airboat in the Everglades?
Airboat experiences are available in the wider Everglades region. Within Everglades National Park, airboats are restricted to designated areas and authorised concession routes.
What wildlife might you see?
The Everglades ecosystem supports alligators, crocodiles, birds, manatees and many other species. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.
