❤️ Our Everglades Experience

Not quite CSI: Miami — but loads of alligators!

We'll admit it: part of us half expected to arrive in the Everglades and find Horatio Caine standing dramatically in the middle of the wetlands putting on his sunglasses. Sadly, CSI: Miami did not materialise. 😂

What we did get was far better than simply looking at the scenery. We headed out across the water on an airboat, skimming over the wetlands and keeping an eye out for wildlife. And unlike our bear-spotting efforts in the Smokies, this time the wildlife cooperated — we spotted loads of alligators.

After the airboat ride, we went to an alligator-handler show, which was great fun and gave us the close-up encounter you can see in our own YouTube Short below. We also had photographs taken with baby alligators.

Then it was back towards Miami, where the day continued with our city tour — Star Island, celebrity homes, Cuban Miami and colourful wall art. Wetlands and alligators in one half of the day; Miami city life in the other.

No Horatio. No sunglasses. But loads of alligators, an airboat and a brilliant South Florida experience — we'll take it!

🚤 What Our Everglades Tour Was Like

Airboat first, alligator show afterwards

For us, the tour worked because it had two very different parts. First came the airboat ride out across the wetlands, where the landscape opens up and you can watch for alligators in their natural environment.

Afterwards came the alligator-handler show and the chance for a much closer encounter. That combination made the visit feel much more substantial than simply stopping at the edge of the Everglades for a photograph.
