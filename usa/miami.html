❤️ Our Miami Experience

The end of the road

Miami felt significant simply because of what it represented: we'd made it all the way from New York to South Florida.

By then our route had taken us through Washington DC and Philadelphia, the mountains of Virginia and Tennessee, Nashville, Memphis, New Orleans, Kennedy Space Center and the Everglades. Finishing among the palm trees of Miami was a fitting contrast to where the journey had begun.

New York to Miami — one enormous road trip and a brilliant way to see how dramatically the USA changes as you travel south.

Rather than padding this guide with attractions we may not have visited, this page is about Miami as we experienced it and its place at the end of the wider journey.
