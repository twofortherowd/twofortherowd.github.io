❤️ Our Memphis Experience

One of the highlights of the entire road trip

After Nashville, we continued west across Tennessee to Memphis. The musical theme continued, but Memphis had its own character — and two names dominated our time here: Beale Street and Graceland.

Graceland was our standout moment of the whole Deep South trip.

That's saying something on a journey that also included Washington DC, the Blue Ridge Mountains, Nashville, New Orleans, the Gulf Coast, Kennedy Space Center and the Everglades. Memphis genuinely delivered one of those travel experiences we'd wanted to have for years.

👑 Graceland
👑 ELVIS PRESLEYOUR TRIP HIGHLIGHT

The place we'd been waiting for

Visiting Graceland was much more than ticking off another famous American attraction. It became the highlight of our entire Deep South journey.

Seeing Elvis Presley's home and the world surrounding it made the story feel much more tangible than simply knowing the songs and photographs. Our own pictures from the house, grounds and memorial are some of the images that best capture our Memphis experience.

Our verdict Graceland was the standout experience of this trip for us. If Memphis is on your itinerary because of its musical history, this is the place we'd make time for.
🎵 Beale Street

Memphis beyond Graceland

Beale Street gave us another side of Memphis and connected perfectly with the musical thread running through this section of our road trip. Nashville had country music; Memphis brought its own blues and rock 'n' roll identity before we continued towards the jazz of New Orleans.
