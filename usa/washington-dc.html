❤️ Our Washington DC Experience

A full day surrounded by American history

After travelling down from New York via Philadelphia, we had a full day to explore Washington DC. The itinerary packed in some of the city's biggest landmarks: the Capitol, Supreme Court, Library of Congress, Lincoln Memorial and the White House.

It was a very different kind of day from the music cities and southern landscapes that came later. Washington was about monuments, institutions and the enormous amount of history concentrated into one place.

Arlington National Cemetery was one of the most moving places we visited on the whole trip.

That visit is the part that stayed with us most strongly. Rather than trying to make this page a generic guide to everything Washington has to offer, we're keeping it focused on the Washington we actually experienced.

🕊️ Arlington National Cemetery

The stop that stayed with us

Of everything we saw around Washington, Arlington National Cemetery was the place we described afterwards as one of the most moving locations of the whole road trip.

That's why it deserves its own section here rather than being buried in a checklist of Washington landmarks. Some travel experiences are memorable because they're fun; others stay with you because they make you stop and think. Arlington was firmly the latter for us.

Our approach to WashingtonWith only a full day, we concentrated on the major historical and political sights rather than trying to cover every museum and neighbourhood in the city.
