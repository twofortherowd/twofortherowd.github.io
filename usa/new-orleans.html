❤️ Our New Orleans Experience

Three music cities, three completely different personalities

By the time we reached New Orleans, we'd travelled through two other cities synonymous with American music: Nashville and Memphis. Yet New Orleans didn't feel like a repeat of either.

That progression became one of the things we loved about this section of the road trip. Nashville brought country music, Memphis gave us Beale Street and Graceland, and then New Orleans added another distinct Deep South atmosphere before our journey continued towards the Gulf Coast and Florida.

Nashville → Memphis → New Orleans became one of the strongest stretches of our entire USA itinerary.

As with our other city pages, we're keeping this centred on what we genuinely experienced and photographed rather than filling it with a generic list of attractions we may not have visited.
