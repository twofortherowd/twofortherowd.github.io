the trip that had everything

02
The story
What a country. What a contrast. What a trip.

This was part of an around-the-world trip we did with friends — flying from Singapore into Perth and eventually making our way home via the Northern Territory, New Zealand, Honolulu and LA. Australia took up a huge chunk of that adventure and delivered something completely new at every single stop.

The East Coast tour — Cairns, the reef, K'gari, the Whitsundays, the Gold Coast, Byron Bay, Brisbane, Sydney and the road south to Melbourne — gave us one memorable stop after another. Then the Northern Territory, which we did independently after New Zealand, added an entirely different dimension: Alice Springs, the Royal Flying Doctor Service and three nights at Uluru.

We also spent time in Tasmania — which completely caught us off guard and turned out to be one of the most jaw-dropping places on the entire trip. And from Australia we continued the adventure into New Zealand — which is covered in its own page.

03
The highlights
The moments that stayed with us
🐠
Great Barrier Reef — two dives

One of our absolute favourite experiences of the entire trip. We did two dives and absolutely loved seeing the colour, marine life and sheer scale of the reef from underwater.

🏝️
K'gari (Fraser Island)

The world's largest sand island — utterly gorgeous. Freshwater lakes among vast sand dunes, wild dingoes and a landscape unlike anything else we saw in Australia.

Our catamaran day in the Whitsundays was one of the standout days of the trip — beautiful water, islands and the famous Whitehaven Beach.

We saw the Twelve Apostles by helicopter and absolutely loved it. Seeing the limestone stacks and the Great Ocean Road coastline from the air gave us a completely different perspective.

Going to see an opera at the Sydney Opera House is one of those things you do because you absolutely have to — and it completely delivered. Inside is as extraordinary as outside.

Missed the cast on the first visit. Went back the next day. Saw Brax. Completely worth it. Absolutely delighted. No further explanation required. 😄

Melbourne & the little penguins

We loved Melbourne's food, cafés and city atmosphere, but seeing Phillip Island's little penguins waddling up the beach at dusk was one of the most memorable parts of our visit.

The most unforgettable part of the entire Australian adventure. Three nights gave time to really experience the place — watching the light change, understanding the significance and feeling the extraordinary silence of the Red Centre.

Where it started
Perth — the most isolated city on earth

The Australian adventure began in Perth — flying in from Singapore, our first stop on Australian soil. 06
The East Coast tour
K'gari, the Whitsundays, Gold Coast & Byron Bay

K'gari (Fraser Island) was gorgeous — the world's largest sand island, with freshwater lakes sitting incongruously in the middle of sand dunes, dingoes roaming the beaches and a landscape that looks like it shouldn't exist but absolutely does. A brilliant stop that often gets overlooked.

The Whitsundays were everything they promised. Out on a catamaran in the middle of some of the most beautiful water in the world — the Coral Sea, dotted with islands, the impossibly white sand of Whitehaven Beach in the distance. One of those days where you keep stopping to make sure what you're seeing is actually real.

The Gold Coast was a proper switch of pace — skyscrapers meeting huge beaches, surf culture everywhere, and the day I tried surfing at Surfers Paradise (with mixed but memorable results). Byron Bay then brought the more relaxed East Coast energy — easy-going, sunny and the kind of place that immediately makes you want to slow down. And there was the overnight stay on a ranch too — one of those unexpected tour stops that turned into a brilliant memory. Brisbane rounded out the region, sitting on the river as the natural gateway to south-east Queensland.

07
The Harbour City
Sydney — sunshine, opera & two trips to Palm Beach

Sydney is everything it's supposed to be — the Opera House is as magnificent in person as in every photograph, the harbour is stunning and the sunshine is relentless. Going to see an opera at the Sydney Opera House was a bucket list moment done properly — inside the building is as extraordinary as outside it. Beyond that, Sydney gave us brilliant beach time at Bondi, the harbour walk, and the Blue Mountains as a stunning day out west.

📺 The Palm Beach situation: Went to Palm Beach day one — missed the Home and Away cast. Went back day two. Saw the cast. Saw Brax specifically. Absolutely delighted. Zero regrets about going twice. This is what travel is about. 😄

08
The road south
Canberra, the Great Ocean Road & Melbourne

After Sydney, we rented a car and drove south — stopping in Canberra for a day. Australia's purpose-built capital is genuinely interesting — the parliament buildings, the National War Memorial, the beautiful lake at the heart of the planned city. Then came one of the great moments of the entire trip — a helicopter flight over the Twelve Apostles on the Great Ocean Road. Extraordinary from the ground; on another level from the air.

Melbourne had a character completely different to Sydney, with a brilliant food and café scene. One of our highlights was Phillip Island's little penguins — seeing them waddle up the beach at dusk was genuinely magical.

Having several nights there gave us time to experience the Red Centre at a slower pace and appreciate the cultural significance of Uluru to the Anangu people.

Common questions
Australia FAQ
Is the Great Barrier Reef worth diving?
We absolutely loved it. We did two dives on the Great Barrier Reef and they remain among our favourite experiences of the entire trip. Diving gave us an unforgettable view of the colour, marine life and scale of the reef.
Is Uluru worth the long journey?
For us, yes. We spent three nights at Uluru, which gave us more time to experience the Red Centre and appreciate the cultural significance of the place to the Anangu people.
Is Palm Beach worth visiting for Home and Away fans?
Absolutely — and go twice if you miss the cast on the first visit, like we did! Palm Beach is a beautiful spot in its own right but for anyone who grew up watching Home and Away it is a proper pilgrimage moment. We can confirm Brax was there on the second visit. 😄
Is Alice Springs worth visiting?
We were glad we visited Alice Springs. The Royal Flying Doctor Service museum was a highlight for us, and we enjoyed experiencing the Outback atmosphere before continuing through the Red Centre.
Is Melbourne worth visiting?
We found Melbourne very different from Sydney and loved its food, cafés and city atmosphere. Seeing the little penguins at Phillip Island was one of the highlights of our visit.
Is the Twelve Apostles helicopter worth it?
We did the helicopter flight ourselves and absolutely loved it. Seeing the Twelve Apostles and the Great Ocean Road coastline from the air was one of the standout experiences of our Australia trip.
Can you do Australia on an around-the-world trip?
Australia is one of the great around-the-world trip destinations. Our route ran from Singapore through Perth, across the East Coast, through Tasmania, New Zealand, the Northern Territory and then on to Honolulu and LA. It is one of the classic around-the-world routes and Australia rewards the effort of getting there enormously.
